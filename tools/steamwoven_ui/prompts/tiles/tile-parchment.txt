MATERIAL TILE - PARCHMENT
Size: 1:1 square, as large as you can. References: CUTOUT (a clean cut-out of this parchment from the approved context image; it is the IMAGE TO EDIT).

Make ONE seamless tile of this aged parchment: it must repeat on all four edges without a visible seam, flat, evenly lit, top-down, edge to edge. Keep its printed light grid of fine lines running straight and evenly spaced, with the same line colour and thickness as in the CUTOUT (the grid continues across the tile edges).
Keep exactly the tone and colour of the CUTOUT (same lightness, same warm hue), but the parchment must not be smooth: give it the spread of brightness of old parchment, with soft mottling, faint ageing stains, uneven darker patches and fibre, in the same colour family and without any new colour. Add no new detail: no burnt or darker edge, no folds, no creases as lines, no text, no pictures, no objects, no border, no vignette, no gradient across the tile, no gloss.
The brightness must vary clearly across the tile: several large soft patches of lighter and darker parchment (the patches differ from the average by about 12 per cent of the lightness) and darker ageing stains, so the tile is far from flat, but the average lightness and the colour saturation stay exactly those of the CUTOUT: do not make it more yellow or more orange, and keep the grid lines as pale as in the CUTOUT. Plan the seamless repeat carefully: patches, stains and grid lines leave one edge where they enter the opposite edge.
