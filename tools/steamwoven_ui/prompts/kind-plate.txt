Part: {part_id} - {part_note}. Size: {width} x {height} px. References: ORIGINAL (the original button art, idle frame), SHEET, CONTEXT crop.

Draw one button plate: the metal or stone body and its ring, without any symbol on it (the symbol is a separate glyph) and without a glow or a pressed-in effect that the ORIGINAL frame of the state does not show. Keep the shape and size relations of the ORIGINAL: round, square, oval or ring-in-square. Idle look: matte, with a clean light edge on the upper left. Transparent background, clean edge, no halo. When the part note lists several sizes, draw them on one sheet in a row with equal spacing so they match, each on its own transparent cell. When the part note lists states (idle, pressed), draw each state in its own cell of one sheet, same size, same framing, same centre, side by side with equal spacing and a plain flat background between them, so the states match; the look of a state is the look of the ORIGINAL frame of that state (a pressed plate may have a darker rim without the light edge), nothing more.
