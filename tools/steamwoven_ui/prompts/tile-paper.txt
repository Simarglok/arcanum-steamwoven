MATERIAL TILE - PAPER
Size: 1:1 square, as large as you can. References: CUTOUT (a clean cut-out of this page paper from the approved context image; it is the IMAGE TO EDIT).

Make ONE seamless tile of this old paper: it must repeat on all four edges without a visible seam, flat, evenly lit, top-down, edge to edge.
Keep exactly the tone and colour of the CUTOUT (same lightness, same muted hue), but the page must not be smooth: give it the spread of brightness of old paper, with soft mottling, faint ageing stains, uneven darkening patches, paper fibre and slight unevenness, as a real old page has, in the same colour family and without any new colour. Add no new detail: no folds, no creases as lines, no edges, no text, no lines, no objects, no border, no vignette, no gradient across the tile, no gloss.
The brightness must vary clearly across the tile: several large soft patches of lighter and darker paper (the patches differ from the average by about 10 per cent of the lightness) and darker ageing stains, so the tile is far from flat, but the average lightness and the colour saturation stay exactly those of the CUTOUT (do not brighten it, do not make it more yellow). Plan the seamless repeat carefully: patches and stains leave one edge where they enter the opposite edge.
