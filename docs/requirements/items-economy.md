# Items and economy — functional requirements

**Current status:** the status of each requirement of this document, the implemented scope, the remainder and the documentation are in the [coverage matrix](coverage/items-economy.md) (rechecked 2026-10-20, ARC-DEV-027). The "Steamwoven" fields below and the summary coverage section give details as of the date of their last edit (initial audit 2026-10-09, merged main [`a8a553b7`](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/tree/a8a553b7c406ace77ea019bf2b13251f00ecddb6), PR #54); where they differ, the matrix is correct.

**Research snapshot:** 2026-10-02. **Scope:** items as objects owned by actors or containers, inventory, equipment, carried resources, item use and state, written objects, trade, theft, trap devices and technology assembly. This is a domain section of the full catalogue, not an audit of all game systems.

**Domain boundaries.** This document records item consequences and links between subsystems. Formulas for attributes, skills, aptitude and training belong to [character and skills](characters-skills.md); damage calculations, ammunition use per attack, spell effects and combat actions belong to [combat, magic and technology](combat-magic-tech.md); NPC reactions, factions and social consequences belong to [story and social systems](story-social-ai.md). The requirements below refer to those domains where an item operation depends on them, without duplicating their formulas.

**Claim levels.** "Original functions" are function bodies and callers of retail `Arcanum.exe` 1.0.7.4 read at the addresses in parentheses; the basis is reading the original's functions at the given addresses, level F0 (not checked in the exe) if there is no row in the verification ledger. Rows of the [verification ledger](../research/original-verification-ledger.md) (EQ, WB, SK, LM) are behavior verified in 1.0.7.4. "Project proposal" is an independent-implementation policy open to discussion. "Unknown" keeps an area where reading functions, limited data reading or the absence of a runtime give no basis for a conclusion. The proposed checks were not run here.

**Steamwoven base:** local `main` `b1245e59c585c7b7b03dd0b764e2d3e31d0f89ae` (PR #36). The project status catalogue lags by up to PR #30; the assessment below is based on the current source snapshot, not on the old status.

## Requirements

<a id="fr-item-001"></a>

### FR-ITEM-001 — Item ownership, containers and moving

**Proposed phase: Pre-alpha.** An item must have exactly one owner or be in the world. The player can view the inventory of the character and of an accessible container, and pick up, carry, rearrange and drop items; the item stays the same instance after a successful move. An object must not be lost or duplicated when a move is refused because of capacity, a script veto or an unsuitable slot. The inventory model distinguishes the container/character grid, equipment slots and quick slots.

**Original functions:** item transfer (0x461810) checks `SAP_GET`, computes an allowed place and calls pickup/insert; drop (0x461AB0) checks `SAP_DROP` and the removal veto, clears the owner and places the object in the world. Insert check and insert (0x466510, 0x466640) and forced removal (0x467860) maintain the container or critter list, the parent, the grid position, pickup/drop/remove triggers and the UI notification. When a direct transfer is impossible, the UI may find another free place. Transfer and pickup (0x4617F0, 0x461810), drop (0x461A70, 0x461A90, 0x461AB0), insert/remove (0x466510, 0x466640), inventory UI (0x572240, 0x572340, 0x572370).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** object model, containers, map placement, weight/space checks and script hooks. On a synthetic inventory, move an item between characters, into a container, into the world and back; fill the grid; check that a refusal keeps the initial owner and a successful operation leaves exactly one record.

**Steamwoven:** partial — the [session inventory](../world/inventory-runtime.md) (ARC-WORLD-014) moves Crash Site items to the hero by pickup from the ground, by searching a dead NPC and by negative IN through the dialog port, and keeps the list of what was taken (schema 5); it keeps NPC inventory changes by NPC identity — added created items and removals visible to searching and `IN` ([ARC-WORLD-055](../world/inventory-runtime.md#npc-inventory-changes-arc-world-055), schema 29); the AREA viewer (ARC-UI-004) picks up and searches by click and shows a diagnostic inventory window on `I`; the insert check (weight, Technophobia, space) is answered by the host; dropping to the ground, stacks, keys and chests are not modeled. Area stores bounded item/container identities and the insertion disposition. See [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). **Unknown:** the original blocking rules, drag-and-drop UI, multiplayer authority and the full set of `SAP_*` restrictions need separate verification.

<a id="fr-item-002"></a>

### FR-ITEM-002 — Weight, capacity and overload consequences

**Proposed phase: Pre-alpha.** Pickup checks the character's carried weight, and the inventory recomputes the consequence of adding and removing an item. Containers and characters have different grid limits; the carried-weight limit is a property of the character and must use the shared stat calculation. The UI explains a refusal when the limit is exceeded and shows the current consequence of equipment/load.

**Original functions:** item weight (0x461370) reads the weight and applies owner modifiers; gold has zero weight in this calculation. Total weight (0x461410) sums the contents; the insert check (0x466510) compares the new weight with `STAT_CARRY_WEIGHT`; a successful insert/removal calls an encumbrance recomputation. Weight of one item and of an inventory (0x461370, 0x461410), pickup gate (0x466510), recomputation on insert/removal (0x466640).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** the carried-weight limit and its change — [character and skills](characters-skills.md); grid and item weight — FR-ITEM-001. Check a successful pickup at the boundary, a refusal one synthetic step above the limit, zero weight of coins and the encumbrance change after removing a heavy item. Do not fix the attribute formula in this document.

**Steamwoven:** the runtime calculation and limits are not implemented; the structural item record does not expose the weight game meaning, see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). **Unknown:** the conversion of the source weight units and the exact link of encumbrance levels to movement/actions must be confirmed separately.

<a id="fr-item-003"></a>

### FR-ITEM-003 — Stacks and partial quantity

**Proposed phase: Pre-alpha.** For items that by their type are quantity resources, moving a chosen quantity decreases the source by the same value and adds it to the recipient or creates a separate world object; a zero quantity does not change the state. Compatible stacks merge, incompatible instances stay separate. The interface offers a quantity choice only where the item supports quantity transfer.

**Original functions:** the stack field selector (0x462410) returns a stack field only for gold and ammunition types. The insert check and insert (0x466510, 0x466640) merge a matching prototype; gold transfer (0x464830) and ammunition transfer (0x4658E0) support passing part of the quantity. The inventory UI calls the quantity window for `OBJ_TYPE_GOLD`/`OBJ_TYPE_AMMO` only; a universal splitter for arbitrary items was not found here. Resource stack types (0x462410), merge on insert (0x466640), partial gold transfer (0x464830), partial ammo transfer (0x4658E0), quantity UI (0x5788C0).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** transfer, money, ammunition pools and economic transactions. Move part of a synthetic stack, merge compatible remainders, refuse a transfer larger than available and check that the total is preserved without duplicates. **Steamwoven:** quantity gameplay stacks are not implemented; see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). **Unknown:** which additional source types outside the branches read may behave as quantities.

<a id="fr-item-004"></a>

### FR-ITEM-004 — Keys, rings and item access actions

**Proposed phase: Later.** Obtained keys are kept as item data; a key ring may collect keys, and the access check uses the character's key and only explicitly supported party-sharing sources. Using a key/lockpick must pass the result to the lock and container system; the rules of lockpicking and alarms belong to separate mechanics.

**Original functions:** item functions extract keys directly from the inventory and the key-ring array, match them with a lock ID and look for keys on the leader/followers; item use in the interface (0x571CB0) passes a lockpick through a skill check, without modeling success itself. Key and key ring search (0x463110, 0x4631A0, 0x463240, 0x463340), party lookup (0x463370), lockpick item → skill caller (0x571CB0).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** locks/containers, party and skills — [character and skills](characters-skills.md), [world and transitions](world.md). Check one matching/non-matching key, a key ring, absence of access and passing the lockpick action to the shared skill runner. **Steamwoven:** the game key ring and opening locks are not implemented; see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). **Unknown:** the key search branches (0x463240–0x463370) contain, at F0, special wildcard/key-sharing rules; the full original coverage is not established.

<a id="fr-item-005"></a>

### FR-ITEM-005 — Equipment and slot legality

**Proposed phase: Pre-alpha.** The player can equip and remove an item to and from the matching slot; a replaced item returns to the grid if removal and placement are allowed. The checks take into account the slot type, clothing model/size compatibility, sex restrictions and hand availability. A fixed two-handed weapon is incompatible with a shield; a damaged item must not be equipped. Changing equipment updates the visible representations of the character and item triggers.

**Original functions:** slot placement (0x464AE0) removes/inserts the item and, on refusal, returns both items to their places ([EQ5](../research/ledger/items.md#equipment-arc-world-028)); the wear check (0x464D20) checks in order the form, Magick Allergy, broken art, the slot, a fixed two-handed weapon and a shield, crippled hands, and armor size and sex ([EQ1](../research/ledger/items.md#equipment-arc-world-028)); the check for removing a worn item (0x465010) always allows it, a refusal for a hexed item is never returned ([EQ2](../research/ledger/items.md#equipment-arc-world-028)). Wear/unwear operations update Art, light and scripted triggers (F0). Slot choice and swap (0x464AE0), equipment restrictions and the removal check (0x464D20, 0x465010), wear/unwear callers (0x4677B0, 0x467860, 0x467CB0).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** slots and actor state; numeric bonuses/armor/combat formulas belong to the combat and character sections. Check the correct replacement of a weapon and clothing, refusal of a two-handed weapon with a shield, a wrong size, a damaged item, return of the replaced item and the update of the external state. **Steamwoven:** [ARC-WORLD-028](../world/equipment-runtime.md) — equipment rules and the hero session domain (put on, replace, take off; snapshot schema 12), without a paperdoll and a host; see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). **Original (ARC-WORLD-028, [EQ1–EQ5](../research/ledger/items.md#equipment-arc-world-028)):** the removal check (0x465010) in retail 1.0.7.4 always allows removal (there is no hexed veto); a shield is forbidden already with one crippled hand ([EQ1](../research/ledger/items.md#equipment-arc-world-028)).

<a id="fr-item-006"></a>

### FR-ITEM-006 — Weapons, ammunition pools and item properties of armor

**Proposed phase: Pre-alpha.** A weapon with an ammunition type can be used only with a sufficient resource; spending and the combat result agree with one ammo pool of the character. Weapon slots/types, armor/clothing properties and the bonuses claimed by the item come from the item data. Any rules for hit, consumption per shot and damage are implemented and verified in [combat, magic and technology](combat-magic-tech.md), and are not repeated here.

**Original functions:** the ammunition count (0x465820) reads the character's pool or a quantity ammo object; ammunition transfer (0x4658E0) moves the exact quantity; best-item selection (0x465170) excludes a weapon without the required ammunition quantity ([WB2](../research/ledger/character.md#starting-kit-arc-world-034)) and uses weapon and skill calculations: damage of five types is summed ([LM1](../research/ledger/character.md#skills-levels-and-character-arc-res-044-arc-res-031-arc-res-027)), the best weapon within range wins ([LM2](../research/ledger/character.md#skills-levels-and-character-arc-res-044-arc-res-031-arc-res-027)). The object field descriptions define separate weapon, ammo and armor fields, but the presence of a field does not confirm specific game data values. Ammo lookup/transfer (0x465820, 0x465870, 0x4658E0, 0x465A40), caller eligibility for best weapon (0x465170), item property fields.

**Dependencies and scenarios:** equip legality (FR-ITEM-005), combat attack gates, ammo inventory and UI quantity. Check synthetically a weapon without ammo, with sufficient/insufficient ammo, ammo transfer and a consistent UI update; leave the combat formulas to the combat report. **Steamwoven:** [ARC-DATA-021](../formats/combat-fields.md) reads the stored weapon, ammo and armor fields (without magic scaling and owner rules); runtime ammo pools and weapon selection are absent, see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). **Unknown:** the stored weapon, ammo and armor fields are read by [ARC-DATA-021](../formats/combat-fields.md); the distributions of values across all data (census ARC-RES-041) and their game meaning were not analyzed here.

### FR-ITEM-007 — Equippable light sources and item effects

**Proposed phase: Pre-alpha.** The light-source property of a carried item affects the owner only while the item is worn in a suitable slot; removing or losing the item recomputes its light. The pickup/use/wear/unwear/attack triggers of an item must invoke the matching effect rule exactly once in the given context. This row records the item integration, while ambient/sector lighting and luminance are defined in the [time and lighting requirements](lighting-time.md).

**Original functions and chain:** wearing (0x4677B0) and removal (0x467CB0) call the light recomputation (0x467E80): the light flags of worn items are shifted by the effective power ratio and merged, and the critter keeps only the largest size flag ([EQ12](../research/ledger/items.md#equipment-arc-world-028)). Insert (0x466640) calls the pickup trigger, and the wear/used pathways dispatch the matching item events. Equip-trigger → recalc (0x4677B0), removal and recalc (0x467CB0, 0x467E70, 0x467E80), item event dispatch (0x4CB7D0, 0x4CB800, 0x4CB830), pickup/used triggers (0x4CBAA0), item used trigger (0x4CBF70).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** FR-ITEM-005, lighting-time, item trigger dispatch and actor light APIs. Wear/remove a synthetic light item and check exactly one expected emitter; use an item with a pickup/use trigger; do not create a second emitter from an object lying in the grid. **Steamwoven:** the light of worn items is a rule of [ARC-WORLD-028](../world/equipment-runtime.md) (effective-power tiers, the largest size), not yet drawn; items with item spells are refused on wear; the wield-on and wield-off scripts of a move run in the session with a script host ([ARC-SCRIPT-042](../world/equipment-runtime.md#wield-events-arc-script-042)), the item-spell trigger graph does not; see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). **Later visual effects** such as modern soft shadows/normal maps remain separate product ideas, not original item behavior.

### FR-ITEM-008 — Identification, name, description and examination

**Proposed phase: Pre-alpha.** An unknown object shows only the unknown-name description intended for it; after identification the player sees the name and the confirmed effects/properties. Examination shows the quantity and only those modifiers that the identification status allows. A paid service must change the identification state and charge the agreed price; the interface and the world record must update together.

**Original functions:** the identification check (0x464370) treats an item with complexity ≤ 0 or with the `OIF_IDENTIFIED` flag as identified, and the price of an unidentified item is 300 ([WB7](../research/ledger/character.md#starting-kit-arc-world-034)); object examination (0x441B60) reads a separate unknown item description before identification. Item examination in the game interface (0x554F10) displays item effects and weapon/armor statistics with unknown modifiers restricted; the identification service (0x467440) sets the flag and transfers money. The description functions (0x4D40D0–0x4D4260), item effects (0x4D3F60–0x4D4080) and internal names (0x4E72B0–0x4E7400) provide strings/internal names from message resources; a full caller chain for reading the internal name (0x4E7400) is not established here. Identification (0x464370, 0x4643D0), unknown name in examine (0x441B60), examine item effects and quantity (0x554F10), identify service (0x467440), string providers (0x4D3F60, 0x4D3FE0, 0x4D3FF0, 0x4D4050, 0x4D4080), internal object names (0x4E72B0).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** item resources, identification service and trade valuation. For a synthetic magical item, compare the unknown/identified name, the properties before/after, the service cost and the money balance. **Steamwoven:** [ARC-DATA-013](../formats/mes-reader.md) chooses the displayed object name by the object examination chain (0x441B60) and reads its text as bytes from description/gamedesc/gamekey.mes; identification, examination, UI and text display are absent. Area wire fields/appearance identity do not establish item display name/valuation semantics. See [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). **Unknown:** the completeness of the hidden states and the exact original localization.

<a id="fr-item-009"></a>

### FR-ITEM-009 — Item use, effects, consumables and charges

**Proposed phase: Pre-alpha.** The player chooses a valid target or cell, gets a refusal on a wrong target/insufficient requirements, and the item's resource changes only by the result of a defined action. One-time food/scroll or special charges are spent and removed by their own rule; equipped items may react to pickup/use/unwear and other explicitly defined triggers. Skill or spell application itself remains the responsibility of the matching domain.

**Original functions:** the UI activates an item and routes the target through application to an object or cell (0x462CC0, 0x462FC0); these functions dispatch `SAP_USE` and magic/tech/item-use triggers, pass written objects to a separate UI, destroy some food/scroll after use and route lockpick/healing items to the skill path. Heal invocation separately decreases the charges of a generic healing item, destroying the item at zero. UI activation → item use (0x571CB0), use on object/location (0x462CC0, 0x462FC0), healing-item charges (0x4C7160).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** target/range rules, skill invocation, spell system and item effects. Check use-to-self/object/location, refusal on a wrong target, successful/failed charge spending, an item with zero and a trigger on one event. **Steamwoven:** use and runtime effects are absent; see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). **Unknown:** the functions read do not prove a universal spending rule for each prototype; exceptions must be established by callers and data.

<a id="fr-item-010"></a>

### FR-ITEM-010 — Books, notes and other written objects

**Proposed phase: Later.** Items of type book/note/newspaper/telegram/plaque/image open a suitable presentation and link the object with its content, without replacing the book with an ordinary item tooltip. A schematic document is handled separately: if the schematic is not yet known, it is added to the discovered knowledge and the document is consumed; re-reading reports that the knowledge is already there. The content is game data; it is not reproduced here.

**Original functions:** application to an object (0x462CC0) sends a written item to the reading UI (0x4606F0); reading start (0x56BB60) distinguishes the schematic subtype and calls the discovered schematic registration (0x4B0120), and routes the other types to the reading mode. Schematic registration (0x4B0120) records a new ID and destroys the item only on the first opening of a schematic. Use written item caller (0x462CC0), written subtype branch (0x56BB60), written-type presentation (0x56BC00), learn-once behavior (0x4B0120).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** localized written data, UI/media and schematic discovery (FR-ITEM-017). Check ordinary reading of several types, closing and reopening; a newly found document adds exactly one schematic entry, a duplicate is kept and not added again. **Steamwoven:** texts and documents of all kinds (`Arcanum.Data.Written`), item use in the session and the SCR 64/65/114 actions, the "Read" button and the diagnostic reading window in the viewer — [ARC-UI-049](../world/reading-window-runtime.md), checked against the exe (WR1–WR16); a schematic document is registered through [ARC-TECHNOLOGY-003](../world/schematic-knowledge-runtime.md). The use script before reading, the item-spell path and action 94 are not modeled; see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). Game texts do not enter the repository.

<a id="fr-item-011"></a>

### FR-ITEM-011 — Durability, breakage and repair

**Proposed phase: Later.** Damage to an item must keep its current durability and a visible broken state. A broken item cannot be used or worn if its type requires that; the repair service shows the cost, applies the repair and charges the payment. The rules of the Repair skill, the training level, critical outcomes and the exact wear of maximum durability stay in [character and skills](characters-skills.md).

**Original functions:** object destruction (0x43F1C0) puts the item into the destroyed art state, clears the current damage field, tries to take it out of equipment and runs `SAP_BUST`; a second bust deletes the object. The wear check (0x464D20) refuses a destroyed item ([EQ1](../research/ledger/items.md#equipment-arc-world-028)). The NPC repair UI computes the cost from damage/price and forbids repairing a broken item without a master; the repair service (0x4C8E60) passes the outcome to the skill runner and the payment, and repair restores HP and lowers durability on any roll ([SK7](../research/ledger/character.md#skills-levels-and-character-arc-res-044-arc-res-031-arc-res-027)). Read in the exe (ARC-RES-062, [item durability](../research/technology-evidence.md#item-durability-fr-item-011)): maximum = hp_pts + hp_adj, current = maximum − hp_damage, all three fields are stored on the object; the first breakage sets the destroyed bit, zeroes the damage, takes the item off and calls BUST, the second deletes the object; a broken item cannot be worn, sold or taken into a schematic, but can be used (TC9); damage lowers the price only when the PC sells; the repair quote counts the damage twice (TC7). Item break/destruction (0x43F1C0), broken equipment gate (0x464D20), repair quote UI (0x578760), NPC repair transaction (0x4C8E60), repair outcome and durability (0x4C7160).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** common object HP/damage state, skill rules, NPC service and prices. Check partial repair, no-op on full health, break/equip rejection, successful master repair and the item destruction boundary. **Steamwoven:** the rules of durability, breakage, price and the item side of repair and the state of items in the session are implemented without callers ([ARC-WORLD-053](../world/item-durability-runtime.md)); see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). **Unknown:** the behavior was not observed in the game; for TC7 (repair quote) and TC9 (a broken item can be used) the intent is unknown.

<a id="fr-item-012"></a>

### FR-ITEM-012 — Lifetime of dropped items

**Proposed phase: Later.** The conditions for an item to disappear must depend on its flags and position: inventory items persist during ordinary game time, while dropped items may have a configured existence period. Returning an item to ownership cancels or suspends the matching event; special non-decaying and junk-pile cases must not disappear on the ordinary timer. This is a game time event, not weather.

**Original functions:** drop (0x461AB0) places the item and schedules decay (0x468090) after 172800000 ms; the decay check (0x468010) takes into account `OIF_NO_DECAY` and the owner, and the decay event (0x467FC0) deletes an object eligible for decay while processing is active. Transfer cancels decay. Drop → 48-hour event scheduling (0x461AB0), decay eligibility and timer (0x467FC0, 0x468010, 0x468090, 0x468110), pickup cancels decay (0x461810).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** game clock/events, save/load and object ownership; FR-LIGHT time subsystem is a clock dependency only. Synthetically drop ordinary, `NO_DECAY` and junk-pile items; advance game time, pick up before the deadline and make sure a stale event does not delete the returned item. **Steamwoven:** decay of dropped items is implemented in the session and works in the viewer ([ARC-WORLD-062](../world/inventory-runtime.md#item-decay-arc-world-062)); junk piles — [ARC-WORLD-063](../world/inventory-runtime.md#junk-piles-arc-world-063): a drop goes into the pile on the tile or gathers the items lying there into a new one, verified by two readers ([JP1–JP8](../research/ledger/items.md#junk-piles-arc-world-063)); see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). An assembly product that did not fit into the inventory lies on the crafter's tile (ARC-WORLD-070) and, as in the original, does not decay: it is created on the tile, not dropped (ID9). The timer and conditions were verified in the original executable by two readers ([ID1–ID9](../research/ledger/items.md#item-decay-arc-world-062)).

<a id="fr-item-013"></a>

### FR-ITEM-013 — Trade, price valuation and payment

**Proposed phase: Later.** Barter must show the items of both sides, the price and the balance before confirmation; a completed transaction moves exactly the item and the agreed money, while a refusal/insufficient funds keeps the initial inventory. The price depends on the base value, the condition, the buy/sell direction, the merchant's limit and haggling; the relation with the NPC is applied by the shared social/reaction rule, not by a separate item formula.

**Original functions:** the item price (0x461F80) takes into account worth, the NPC retail multiplier, haggle effectiveness and item HP; the worth of an unidentified item is 300, otherwise the stored value is not less than 2 ([WB7](../research/ledger/character.md#starting-kit-arc-world-034)); the sell and buy checks (0x462170, 0x462230) check the legality of the deal, the script/buy-list and stolen flags. The barter UI applies item/gold/ammo changes, checks the remaining funds and rolls back some errors. Price and buy/sell criteria (0x461F20, 0x461F60, 0x461F80, 0x462170, 0x462230), UI transaction checks and transfer (0x578EA0), barter open path (0x572510).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** gold/quantity transfer (FR-ITEM-003), haggling in characters-skills and NPC reaction in story/social/AI. Check both sides of the deal, the exact calculation of funds, a merchant refusal, insufficient money, a damaged item and rollback on an impossible transfer. **Steamwoven:** trade, NPC inventory state and transaction runtime are absent; see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). The reaction function 0x4C1150 is left as an unread dependency; its formula is not fixed here.

<a id="fr-item-014"></a>

### FR-ITEM-014 — Merchant assortment and its refresh

**Proposed phase: Later.** The inventory source data define the possible assortment, the coin range, prototype appearance probabilities and the allowed buy-back categories. An NPC/container uses its own stock state; reopening must not silently duplicate one-time loot. The restock frequency and the differences between a shop and an ordinary NPC/loot source must be defined from specific source records and the original's behavior.

**Original functions:** the InvenSource initialization (0x4BF390) loads `Rules\\InvenSource.mes` and the optional `Rules\\InvenSourceBuy.mes`; records contain coin bounds, prototype + rate and buy-all/allowlist. Item functions generate a container/critter inventory source and schedule out-of-view NPC regeneration through a game interval of 12 to 24 hours, divided by 4 in multiplayer. This regeneration does not prove a universal shop restock policy. Set list retrieval (0x4BF5D0) accepts `id ≤ count`, so the last set is reachable ([LM13](../research/ledger/npc-ai.md#npc-ai-reaction-and-companions-arc-res-039-arc-res-028-arc-res-024)). InvenSource loading and lookup, initialization/get set (0x4BF390, 0x4BF510, 0x4BF5D0), assortment and buy-list parser (0x4BF7B0, 0x4BFAA0), inventory generation/respawn (0x463C60, 0x463E20, 0x4640C0).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** item prototypes/content loading, game clock, container/NPC lifecycle and FR-ITEM-013. Check the source, one-time generation, buy-all/allowlist, keeping what was bought/sold and the real restock rule for a specific merchant profile. **Steamwoven:** a merchant stand-in container in the barter window, transfer of its gold and identification on opening (ARC-WORLD-090, [barter](../world/barter-runtime.md)); the restock and set-generation rules were read in the exe and exist in Simulation (ARC-WORLD-093, `RestockRules`), the restock event exists in the session (ARC-WORLD-094) and in the viewer (ARC-WORLD-096); the seed and generator of one-time containers (flag 0x200) were read in the exe and exist in Simulation (ARC-WORLD-098), but the session does not run them yet; see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage).

<a id="fr-item-015"></a>

### FR-ITEM-015 — Stealing and planting an item

**Proposed phase: Later.** In pickpocket mode an item must not be moved unnoticed by simple drag-and-drop: steal and plant start a skill action that updates the owner only by its outcomes. Stolen property keeps an item flag/provenance; trade checks it, and the exceptions of a fence/permitted buyer are applied explicitly. Social/AI fallout and the chance of theft are not implemented in the item layer.

**Original functions:** the inventory interface distinguishes dragging from the character's side to the target's side and back, then calls steal (0x4C6FD0) or plant (0x4C7010); these wrappers start the shared PICK_POCKET invocation. The sell check (0x462170) forbids selling a stolen item to an ordinary buyer unless the merchant is a fence. Steal/plant UI callers (0x575100, 0x575180), skill wrappers (0x4C6FD0, 0x4C7010), stolen sale restriction (0x462170).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** pickpocket skill and detection/faction reactions — character/social domains; ownership/transaction — FR-ITEM-001/013. Check success, failure, planting, the stolen flag and a sale to a fence vs an ordinary trader. **Steamwoven:** pickpocket inventory mode and provenance are absent; see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). **Unknown:** the consequences of detection and all allowed scripts require a full AI/dialog audit.

<a id="fr-item-016"></a>

### FR-ITEM-016 — Traps and placeable devices as item actions

**Proposed phase: Later.** Using a trap-device item directs the player to a valid target or coordinate; on successful placement the item is consumed and a world device with a linked effect/scenario is created. Some devices may set a use action on a door/container; an attempt to place a second trap in an occupied cell must be refused without losing the item. Detection/disarming passes the result to the skill rules; damage/explosion — combat/world effects.

**Original functions:** application to an object or cell (0x462CC0, 0x462FC0) selects a trap-device (0x4BCA60); placement on an object (0x4BC2E0) checks that the target is valid, and placement in a cell (0x4BC480) checks occupancy and creates a trap prototype/script, destroying the item on success. Disarming (0x4BC7B0) on a successful expert+ critical result may return the item; the skill formula is located in a separate invocation. Item caller into trap path (0x462CC0), device on object/location (0x4BC2E0, 0x4BC480), disarm result and recovered item (0x4BC7B0, 0x4BC9C0).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** target/location placement, item-use triggers, trap state, combat effect and disarm skill. Synthetically place a device on an unsupported target and an occupied place, check no-consume on rejection, consume/create on success, single disarm and optional recovered item. **Steamwoven:** dynamic trap placement/actions are absent; see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). The section read does not establish all original trap/device prototypes.

<a id="fr-item-017"></a>

### FR-ITEM-017 — Found and unlockable technology schematics

**Proposed phase: Later.** The player can browse the available schematics by technology discipline: found documents and schematics opened by the current degrees. Found entries are stored separately from the learned degree; receiving a found schematic again does not create a duplicate. The book shows the required items, the product, the expertise gate and readiness to assemble. Tech degree/intelligence progression stays a dependency of [character and skills](characters-skills.md).

**Original functions:** found schematic registration (0x4B0120) reads the ID from the written item and adds only a new ID to `OBJ_F_PC_SCHEMATICS_FOUND_IDX`; the schematic window switch (0x56D130) separately forms the learned count by tech degree and the found list; the ID of a learned schematic is derived from tech/degree (0x4B0320). Reading schematic data (0x56DBD0) loads recipe fields; the schematic discipline (0x56DB00) is determined for both categories. Found schematic registration (0x4B0120), learned/found lists in UI (0x56D130), learned IDs and grouping (0x56DB00, 0x56DB60), recipe field model/load (0x56DBD0).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** written items (FR-ITEM-010), tech disciplines and schematics content. Check learned vs found tabs, a new document, a repeated document, empty discipline and a recipe with multiple interchangeable component prototypes. **Steamwoven:** schematic data are read (ARC-TECHNOLOGY-001, [contract](../formats/schematics.md): `tech.mes` texts, records, ranges, discipline, selection and stub); degrees in the session ([ARC-TECHNOLOGY-002](../world/tech-degrees-runtime.md)), the learned and found tabs, the hero's found list and document registration ([ARC-TECHNOLOGY-003](../world/schematic-knowledge-runtime.md)); the schematic window in the viewer shows the tabs ([ARC-TECHNOLOGY-005](../world/schematic-window-runtime.md)), a document is registered by the inventory "Read" button ([ARC-UI-049](../world/reading-window-runtime.md)); see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). Read in the exe and recounted from the data (ARC-RES-062, [technology research](../research/technology-evidence.md#learned-and-found-lists)): a schematic record is 7 rows of `rules\schematic.mes`, 56 learned (7 per discipline, the first "stored degree" entries are visible), 62 found, 8 stubs; a found document of subtype 5 is added once and destroyed.

<a id="fr-item-018"></a>

### FR-ITEM-018 — Assembling a device from a schematic

**Proposed phase: Later.** Assembly is available when the character has the required expertise and each mandatory component from the alternative prototype choices. Success consumes the components in the given quantity and creates the configured result of the given quantity in the inventory; a shortage of a resource/expertise consumes nothing. Item discipline/complexity define the scope of the recipe gate, but do not replace the shared progression calculation.

**Original functions:** the schematic UI gets materials by matching prototypes and checks for each group the item and the effective technology degree (0x4B00B0) against the discipline and the negative item complexity; assembly (0x56E720) finds both components, spends one unit of ammo or destroys an ordinary ingredient and creates product objects in the quantity from the schematic data, passing them to the inventory. A potential failure to create the product after the ingredients are consumed is visible in the sequence read and must be covered separately in an independent transactional model. Component and expertise gate (0x56E190), assembly process and consumption (0x56E720), discipline level API (0x4B00B0, 0x4B0110).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** FR-ITEM-017, inventory capacity/ownership (FR-ITEM-001/002), tech degree rules and recipe data. Check item/expertise gates, an alternative prototype, an ammo ingredient, a successful output count, missing components and a full inventory; on an output failure, do not lose the resources already committed. **Steamwoven:** recipe data (components, product, quantity) are read (ARC-TECHNOLOGY-001, [contract](../formats/schematics.md)); the component gate and the hero's assembly in the session without losses — [ARC-TECHNOLOGY-004](../world/schematic-assembly-runtime.md); the hero's schematic window in the viewer with an assembly button — [ARC-TECHNOLOGY-005](../world/schematic-window-runtime.md); the NPC crafter (`su`, action 129 for an NPC) — [ARC-TECHNOLOGY-006](../world/schematic-assembly-runtime.md#npc-crafter-arc-technology-006): components from the NPC's list, products into its list, as in the original ([SW7, NC6](../research/ledger/technology.md#npc-schematic-crafter-arc-technology-006)); see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). **Read in the exe (ARC-RES-062, [assembly](../research/technology-evidence.md#assembly-0x56e720-fr-item-018-f2)):** the gate looks for a component only in the crafter's own inventory (exact prototype, no worn and no broken ones), expertise in the component's discipline ≥ −complexity; the assembly itself does not recheck the gate (TC5), consumes the components before creating the product (TC3) and has no roll, skill, AP or experience; in a dialog (`su`) the crafter is an NPC. The button state codes (O7) were closed by the third reading (ARC-TECHNOLOGY-004).

<a id="fr-item-019"></a>

### FR-ITEM-019 — Gambling and an item stake with an NPC

**Proposed phase: Pre-alpha.** In trade mode the player can offer an explicitly chosen item from the NPC's side for a game of chance. Before the roll the interface shows the item, its computed value and the sufficiency of funds/skill; once allowed, the stake changes item ownership, world position, money and the final message consistently and exactly once. An error, cancellation or insufficient eligibility must not change the inventory silently. The Gambling status/training and the chance formulas belong to [character and skills](characters-skills.md); here the UI, stake eligibility and item/economy outcomes are fixed.

**Original functions:** a special gambling zone is enabled only in the barter UI. A chosen item from the PC's side is rejected; the NPC must have Gambling points; the maximum item value is compared with the player's limit, and the player is checked for having money. For a worn/`OIF_WONT_SELL` item the Gambling training of level Expert/Master is checked, and in the original both tiers read the **PC's** training ([SK5](../research/ledger/character.md#skills-levels-and-character-arc-res-044-arc-res-031-arc-res-027)). After that the inventory caller runs `SKILL_GAMBLING` with the PC, the NPC and the item as arguments. The skill check uses a next roll saved in advance, which is stored in the save (0x4C5DE0 saves, 0x4C5DC0 loads), so repeating by loading does not change the outcome. The caller picks flags `9` on success and `0x11` on failure (read again at F1 in ARC-UI-1740: the shared transfer handler 0x578EA0 gives the item with a gift of minus its worth on `9`, and with `0x10` pays the price as for a buy): a win takes the item for nothing, a loss costs its price, and the merchant's reaction on a win is the item's worth taken as a gift ([ledger](../research/ledger/items.md#gambling-bets-arc-ui-1740)). Barter mode creates gambling zone (0x5727B0), eligibility and displayed checks (0x579840), zone hover and input caller (0x573D61), item-to-skill invocation and outcome flags (0x579B60), generic item/payment outcome route (0x578EA0), saved gambling roll and skill limit (0x4C5DC0, 0x4C5DE0), level-based item-cost cap (0x4C6520, 0x4C6560), non-retry roll caller (0x4C7160).

**Dependencies and scenarios:** barter pricing (FR-ITEM-013), inventory/ownership (FR-ITEM-001), [Gambling rank and invocation rules](characters-skills.md), [barter UI](ui-media-lifecycle.md), saved-roll boundary (FR-LIFECYCLE-003). Proposed checks: the wrong inventory side, the NPC lacking the skill, an item value above the allowed limit, insufficient money, a worn and a `WONT_SELL` item at each training boundary; separately check a single debit/credit of funds and placement of the item for each outcome, no duplicates, a correct update after loading and no reroll through save/reload. **Steamwoven:** the bet gate, the bet, the saved roll and the bet box of the barter window exist (ARC-UI-1740); see [coverage](#current-steamwoven-coverage). **Unknown:** the original runtime was not observed for a bet (the oracle does not run the barter window).
