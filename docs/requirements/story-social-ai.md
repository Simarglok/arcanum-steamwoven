### FR-DIALOG-001 — Starting a conversation with a suitable participant

**Product goal (for review):** start a conversation with the chosen NPC from the assigned dialog point, without a second conflicting window; if there is no assignment, the product may show the intended short reply. **Boundary:** start, admission check and handover of control. **Original:** `SAP_DIALOG` from the object handler reaches the start of the dialog in the UI (0x567460); the UI checks the PC state and whether the NPC can speak, selects the DLG by script number and opens the node. **Dependency / unknown:** the selected NPC→SCR→DLG links are researched and supported by the bounded entry bridge; real conversation admission/lifecycle, the state service and original-runtime parity remain unchecked. **Acceptance (proposal):** two NPCs with different assignments open different records; a blocking state does not open a conversation. **Steamwoven:** partial: [NpcDialogEntryResolver](../scripting/npc-dialog-entry-runtime.md) computes the checked NPC DLG node from the effective SAP_DIALOG/SCR and versioned supplied queries. The caller does not select the entry node. Life/combat/exclusivity admission, world interaction, DialogSession.Start/effects and UI are not connected. [PR #48](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/pull/48). ARC-SCRIPT-005: the computed entry starts the [conversation runtime](../scripting/dialog-conversation-runtime.md); the AREA viewer opens a conversation with a left click on an NPC within less than 5 cells (the original's talk range, goal 21; for a distant NPC the hero's walk goal leads it next to the NPC first, ARC-DEV-169). There is still no admission by life, combat and occupancy. **Sources:** start of the dialog in the UI (0x567460) · call of the object's SCR (0x441980) · the SCR action passes context to the UI (0x447220) · [binding boundaries](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/blob/b1245e59c585c7b7b03dd0b764e2d3e31d0f89ae/docs/scripting/dialog-transition-runtime.md#L5-L15).

### FR-DIALOG-002 — Availability of lines and their order

**Product goal (for review):** answers go in the order of the dialog data; only the options whose conditions hold for the current PC, NPC and story state are visible. **Boundary:** the choice of availability is separate from the text display and from executing the result. **Original:** the enumeration builds up to five options; the conditions checked include gender, IQ, stats, skills, alignment, flags and counters, quests, reaction, reputation, items, rumors and other context fields. **Unknown:** these are selectively read branches, not a complete list of rules; DLG verification and a check of the original runtime are needed. **Dependencies:** character, quests, items and social state. **Acceptance:** change one condition at a time in a synthetic matrix and check visibility and order. **Steamwoven:** partial — the [conversation runtime](../scripting/dialog-conversation-runtime.md) checks gender, IQ (with the dumb rule), then the conjunction LF/GF/RE/RA/ME (`ME` is "met before" through the session port, ARC-WORLD-043) in the order of the numeric-ID group and stops at five eligible; PC facts and reaction are supplied by the caller (in the viewer, a diagnostic profile), other conditions are the frontier. **Sources:** the option enumeration (0x414F50) · start of the condition check (0x4150D0) · end of the condition check (0x4150D0) · [parameters set by the caller](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/blob/b1245e59c585c7b7b03dd0b764e2d3e31d0f89ae/docs/scripting/dialog-transition-runtime.md#L56-L71).

### FR-DIALOG-003 — Text variants, contextual answers and NPC lines

**Product goal (for review):** show the NPC line and the PC options chosen by the dialog context; support participant names and the intended contextual variants. **Boundary:** specific source phrases, codecs, voice-over and localizations are not defined here. **Original:** a DLG record (field parsing checked in the exe, row [DL1](../research/ledger/scripts.md#scripts-event-queue-and-randomness-arc-res-042-arc-res-045-arc-script-007-arc-res-046-arc-world-019)) contains text fields, a condition, an answer and an action; helpers choose PC/NPC variants by context, a separate function substitutes names and overrides the NPC line; the UI shows the current exchange. **Unknown:** the source strings, speech IDs and the distribution of variants were not studied. **Dependencies:** localized text resources and the chosen DLG record. **Acceptance:** a synthetic gender/IQ/race matrix selects the expected text and names without side effects. **Steamwoven:** partial — the [conversation runtime](../scripting/dialog-conversation-runtime.md) decodes Windows-1252, selects the male/female NPC line, substitutes @pcname@/@npcname@ and types generated markers; 32 files of generic `gd_*` phrases are loaded through the content stack by the exe's rules (ARC-DATA-042, [files](../formats/generated-dialog-files.md)); the NPC greeting `g:` is built from them per the exe ([greeting](../scripting/dialog-greeting.md), ARC-WORLD-058), the markers `i:`, `m:`, `r:` are not yet; the AREA viewer panel shows the line and the options. **Sources:** DLG record fields (0x417860) · contextual text variants (0x4182D0, 0x418390, 0x418460, 0x418480, 0x4185F0, 0x418780) · names and NPC line override (0x418870, 0x4189C0) · display of the line/options (0x5684C0) · [DLG parser boundaries](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/blob/b1245e59c585c7b7b03dd0b764e2d3e31d0f89ae/docs/scripting/dialog-transition-runtime.md#L11-L15).

### FR-DIALOG-004 — Result of a choice, exits and special actions

**Product goal (for review):** the chosen answer applies the declared consequences and then continues the conversation, closes it or opens the required screen/script. **Boundary:** transitions and routing of effects; the rules of the subsystems it hands control to belong to their domains. **Original:** the answer handler changes reaction, quest state, flags, alignment, rumors, training and the companion roster; the UI chooses the continuation, the exit or a special handover of control. The actions `RU`/`RQ` are checked in the exe (row [LB12](../research/ledger/ui-audio.md#logbook-arc-ui-008)). This describes the transition, but not the contract of the target subsystems. Conditions of access to gambling may arise in the dialog; the bet, the exchange of items and the game screen belong to [FR-ITEM-019](items-economy.md). **Unknown:** the semantics of all codes and refusals is not checked against the original. **Dependencies:** quest state, social data and the UI of the target screens. **Acceptance:** each transition type causes exactly one expected continuation; an erroneous address leaves no partial effect. **Steamwoven:** partial — [conversation runtime](../scripting/dialog-conversation-runtime.md): Direct, Exit and a negative answer as a typed SCR line (not executed), JO/FL, effects LF/GF/QU/RU/RE/AL/IN/MM with receipts; insult `i:` (ARC-WORLD-043, 0x4191E0): after the answer's action −10 to reaction, at reaction ≤ 20 the NPC ends the conversation without an SCR continuation, otherwise the answer's transition; barter/training etc. are shown as blocked without effects. **Sources:** answer selection and its effects (0x415BA0) · story and companion roster changes (0x415BA0) · handover from the UI and SCR continuation (0x567E30, 0x5680A0, 0x5681C0, 0x568430, 0x568480) · [transition categories in Steamwoven](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/blob/b1245e59c585c7b7b03dd0b764e2d3e31d0f89ae/docs/scripting/dialog-transition-runtime.md#L45-L54).

### FR-SCRIPT-001 — Events and the role of the script initiator

**Product goal (for review):** a script runs from an assigned event for an object, a cell or a sector and receives stable roles of initiator, script owner, additional object and attachment point. **Boundary:** assignment and handover of context; the map and the physical detection of the event belong to the world domain. **Original:** the list of events is 36 SAPs (0..35, F0); the object dispatcher selects the script by `attachee` and the attachment point (`attachment point`) and saves the changed script number, flags and counters. The tile handler runs `SAP_USE`, and the PC entering a sector invokes the sector script and the event for companions. Changes to a cell's own script are not saved, those to a sector's script are saved (rows [X3](../research/ledger/scripts.md#scripts-event-queue-and-randomness-arc-res-042-arc-res-045-arc-script-007-arc-res-046-arc-world-019) and [TR5](../research/ledger/world.md#map-transitions-arc-data-025)). **Unknown:** the source bindings of maps, prototypes and actors were not studied. **Acceptance:** synthetic events pass the right roles and do not invoke a neighboring SAP. **Steamwoven:** partial: the production NPC bridge reads the whole-array effective SAP_DIALOG attachment and the typed event/participants/version context. This is one explicit invocation, without a general SAP dispatcher, callback lifecycle and attachment writeback. [API](../scripting/npc-dialog-entry-runtime.md). Research of the general dispatcher (ARC-RES-042): [calls of all SAPs, roles, result and slot write, cuts](../research/scr-event-dispatcher.md). **Sources:** the event list SAP · call of the object's script (0x441980) · event for a cell (0x4C0980) · sector and companion events (0x4F2330).

### FR-SCRIPT-002 — Conditions, branching, actions and managed SCR state

**Product goal (for review):** execute ordered conditions and actions, continue with the next line, a branch, a return or a loop, and resolve references through an explicit script context. **Boundary:** the catalog (F0) contains 53 condition types, 142 actions, 24 `focus-object` types and 7 value types; this is a list of symbols, not a full audit of branches. **Original:** script execution (0x4449B0) performs branch and action (`action`) selection, accesses local and global values, flags, counters and object slots; the loop limit of 1000 iterations is not checked against the exe (F0). Checked against the exe: the transition control codes −1..−4, division, masking of counters to 8 bits and the writing of global flags without a mask (rows [X6–X8, X10](../research/ledger/scripts.md#scripts-event-queue-and-randomness-arc-res-042-arc-res-045-arc-script-007-arc-res-046-arc-world-019)). **Unknown:** the use of each opcode in the resources and the original semantics need separate verification. **Dependencies:** SCR data and the game owners of values. **Acceptance:** a table of implemented opcodes checks order, transitions and state, and an unknown opcode gives an explicit refusal. **Steamwoven:** partial: the NPC bridge supports bounded TRUE/LOCAL_FLAG/FOLLOWING/JILTED and advance/GOTO/DIALOG; the unsupported is frontier/error. The [GF owner/typed adapters](../scripting/global-flag-state-runtime.md) are implemented separately, GF is Unsupported in the one-step DLG runtime but supported in the [conversation runtime](../scripting/dialog-conversation-runtime.md) (ARC-SCRIPT-005); there is no general SCR VM, no world-state ports and no execution of all actions. By the reading of the functions (F0), the original silently skips unknown conditions and actions; the proposed policy of static admission with a typed refusal is in [ARC-RES-042](../research/scr-event-dispatcher.md#7-unknown-commands-source-and-proposed-policy). **Sources:** object and value types · 53 condition types · 142 action types · script execution transitions (0x4449B0) · value mapping (0x44B960, 0x44B9B0, 0x44BA70, 0x44BB50) · [limited DLG command format](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/blob/b1245e59c585c7b7b03dd0b764e2d3e31d0f89ae/docs/scripting/dialog-transition-runtime.md#L73-L104).

## Open questions and limits of confidence

- Script names are divided into a system table (numbers from 1000, at game start) and a module table (1..999, at module load), and the DLG name is built from the SCR name: system (0x4CEC10), module (0x4CECE0, 0x4CEDA0, 0x4CEDC0), DLG path (0x4CEE60); checked in the exe, row [SN1](../research/ledger/scripts.md#scripts-event-queue-and-randomness-arc-res-042-arc-res-045-arc-script-007-arc-res-046-arc-world-019). - The cell script (0x4C0980) runs on a copy and the record is returned unchanged, so a script removing itself is not saved — a bug of the original (rows [X3 tile](../research/ledger/scripts.md#scripts-event-queue-and-randomness-arc-res-042-arc-res-045-arc-script-007-arc-res-046-arc-world-019) and [TR5](../research/ledger/world.md#map-transitions-arc-data-025)).
