# Combat, magic and technology requirements

**Current status:** the status of each requirement in this document, the implemented scope, the remainder and the documentation are in the [coverage matrix](coverage/combat-magic-tech.md) (re-checked 2026-10-20, ARC-DEV-027). The "Steamwoven" fields below and the summary coverage section give the detail as of their last edit (initial audit 2026-10-09, merged main [`a8a553b7`](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/tree/a8a553b7c406ace77ea019bf2b13251f00ecddb6), PR #54); where they differ, the matrix is correct.

Date: 2026-10-02. Initial Steamwoven research baseline: `b1245e59c585c7b7b03dd0b764e2d3e31d0f89ae`. The behavior of the original is given by the addresses of retail `Arcanum.exe` 1.0.7.4 functions in parentheses. Coverage refers only to the pinned Steamwoven baseline. The document is a draft of the full catalogue for owner review and approval, not an approved specification and not a task queue.

Scope: the player starts combat, chooses targets and actions, spends action points, deals and takes damage, applies and sustains magical or technological effects, interacts with active items and sees the corresponding game states. Attributes, skills, aptitude and their development belong to the character section; the equipment model, item ownership, ammunition as inventory content, trade and crafting belong to the items section. Only their inputs into the combat and magic-tech rules are given here. Map switching and long-distance travel from spell effects must be agreed with the separate world section.

**How to read the evidence.** "Original functions" is the described branch or call in a retail exe function at the given address; the basis is a reading of the original's functions at the given addresses, level F0 (not checked in the exe), if there is no row in the verification ledger. The rows of the [verification ledger](../research/original-verification-ledger.md) (TB, RT, FA, MG, EQ, XP and others) are behavior checked in 1.0.7.4. The target scenarios below are proposed observable product requirements. A mechanic without a ledger row stays **original-runtime-unverified**. An unread or doubtful branch is the limit of this reading, not proof that the mechanic is absent from the original game. No spell list, code, texts or game data were extracted or copied.

## Requirements

<a id="fr-combat-001"></a>

### FR-COMBAT-001 — The player switches between real-time and turn-based combat

**Behavior and acceptance.** The player can enter combat, continue it in real-time or switch on turn-based mode, end their turn and leave combat under a permitted condition. If the fast-turn setting is saved, its expected effect on movement, turning, waiting and action availability must be checked separately; the invariance of the action set under it was not established by the functions reviewed. Acceptance: one scenario starts and ends combat in each mode; the log records the mode change and the acting actor without loss of combat state.

**Original functions and data.** Combat initialization (0x4B1D50) registers the settings `turn-based`, `fast turn-based` and `auto attack` (by default the first two are off and auto attack is on; the value from the settings file takes precedence, [RT6](../research/ledger/combat.md#real-time-combat-arc-combat-004)). Mode and fast-turn switching (0x4B6C90, 0x4B6D20, [RT4](../research/ledger/combat.md#real-time-combat-arc-combat-004)), the start of turn-based combat (0x4B71E0, [TB2](../research/ledger/combat.md#turn-based-round-arc-combat-003)) and the fast-continuation condition (0x4B8040) perform the switching, start and end. The animation callers reviewed use the predicate to choose an instant turn or to process several path steps in one turn with the remaining AP (turn (0x42C440), movement and AP (0x42E9B0)). This does not prove the behavior of all actions. Needs mode settings, combatant state, animation/combat rules. **Steamwoven:** the turn-based round (ARC-COMBAT-003) and the combat mode with a toggle, an exit rule and turn-based switching in the session (ARC-COMBAT-004, [contract](../world/real-time-combat-runtime.md)); in the viewer R switches on the real-time combat mode, and the viewer has no turn-based setting.

<a id="fr-combat-002"></a>

### FR-COMBAT-002 — The participant queue and initiative are explainable

**Behavior and acceptance.** Active participants get a stable turn order, an inactive or incapacitated participant is skipped, and the transition to the next round is explicit. Acceptance compares several actors with different parameters and shows the actual order and the reason for a skip; the initiative rule is not fixed until the original behavior is checked.

**Original functions and data.** The original collects PC/NPC in the square `PC ± range` around the local PC, where range is half the effective Perception + 5, at least 10 ([TB1](../research/ledger/combat.md#turn-based-round-arc-combat-003), [TB2](../research/ledger/combat.md#turn-based-round-arc-combat-003)); the move to the next participant goes along a linked list, skipping inactive ones ([TB4](../research/ledger/combat.md#turn-based-round-arc-combat-003), [TB7](../research/ledger/combat.md#turn-based-round-arc-combat-003)), and the end of a turn advances game time by one second ([TB8](../research/ledger/combat.md#turn-based-round-arc-combat-003)) (collection/range (0x4B71E0, 0x4B7300), turn sequence (0x4B7690, 0x4B7740)). The list sort (0x4B7EB0) at F0 moves the PC to the tail and recalculates reaction, but does not sort by initiative. **Steamwoven:** absent ([status][status]); the initiative rule and the initial queue are unknown.

<a id="fr-combat-003"></a>

### FR-COMBAT-003 — Actions are limited by a visible AP budget

**Behavior and acceptance.** Turn-based mode checks and spends AP for movement, melee/ranged attack, cast, skill, item use/pickup; an insufficient budget forbids the action or ends the turn under the rules of the chosen policy. Any permitted overexert must explicitly account for the cost to the character's state. Acceptance checks actions near the AP threshold, stepping away from an adjacent target, end-turn and the displayed remainder.

**Original functions and data.** The AP checks per action, the step and attack cost and the AP deduction define the checks/spending: a step costs 2 AP (1 for a PC with always-run), an attack depends on weapon speed, use costs 2, pickup 1, cast and skill 4 each ([TB10](../research/ledger/combat.md#turn-based-round-arc-combat-003)); the local PC can spend the remaining AP at the price of 2 Fatigue damage ([TB9](../research/ledger/combat.md#turn-based-round-arc-combat-003)) (AP checks/costs (0x4B7790, 0x4B7830, 0x4B78D0, 0x4B79A0, 0x4B7A20, 0x4B7AA0, 0x4B7AE0, 0x4B7BA0, 0x4B7C20, 0x4B7C30, 0x4B7C90, 0x4B7CD0)). Needs weapon fields, movement-cost callers and character skills/states. **Steamwoven:** partial — the round rules and action costs ([turn-based round](../world/turn-based-round-runtime.md)); a cast target spends 4 AP through the same calculation, a refusal ends the target ([cast host](../world/spell-cast-host.md), ARC-MAGIC-014, CG2); there is no budget UI ([status][status]).

<a id="fr-combat-004"></a>

### FR-COMBAT-004 — The action target is validated before application

**Behavior and acceptance.** Before an attack the system checks that the chosen target is valid for the specific action; the target can be a critter, an object or a tile, and the restrictions include type/state/range/area. A cancellation or refusal leaves the target and resources without a side change. Acceptance covers a valid target, a dead one, an invisible/inaccessible one and a tile outside the permitted radius.

**Original functions and data.** The target-context check (0x4F2D20) checks spell/target flags, types, sides, state and tile restrictions; the target-list builder (0x4F40B0) creates object/tile/radius/wall/circle targets. They are called by the game interface's target selection, the spell UI and magic-tech invocation (validation (0x4F2D20), target lists (0x4F40B0), target selection by screen point and callers (0x4F2830), the game interface's cast caller (0x54DE50)). The basis is a reading of the original's functions at the given addresses, level F0 (not checked in the exe), if there is no row in the verification ledger. **Steamwoven:** partial — the [attack resolver](../world/attack-resolver-runtime.md) (ARC-COMBAT-001) refuses an inactive attacker and a dead critter target; there is no general target selection and validation ([status][status]). The cone/count branches, some non-party checks and one radius check stayed doubtful in the reading; they need a separate check in the exe.

<a id="fr-combat-005"></a>

### FR-COMBAT-005 — An attack resolves a hit, a dodge and a miss

**Behavior and acceptance.** A melee/ranged attack goes through the skill/weapon check, target conditions, hit/miss, dodge, aim/backstab and a critical outcome; a hit starts damage only after the check is resolved, and a miss can have its own effect. Acceptance with a fixed seed and actors on both sides of the threshold compares the chosen target, die/result, dodge and the resulting state change.

**Original functions and data.** Attack resolution (0x4B3170) performs the weapon aptitude check, the ranged blocker, the skill check, dodge, Fate crits and then calls the melee/ranged processor. Building the attack context (0x4B2210) from the weapon/skill marks melee/ranged/backstab. Anomalies A1–A10 of this chain were checked in the exe by task ARC-COMBAT-001 ([original check](../research/combat-core-evidence.md#original-executable-check-arc-combat-001)). Needs skills, weapons, states and RNG; the skill formulas themselves belong to the character section. **Steamwoven:** partial — the [attack resolver](../world/attack-resolver-runtime.md) (ARC-COMBAT-001): a pure function with an injected roll source resolves the hit location, technology refusal and magic cancellation, the attack check, dodge, Fate and a critical miss on oneself; synthetic tests on both sides of the thresholds. Skills, weapons and states are supplied by the caller; it is not connected to the session, the round or the viewer ([status][status]).

<a id="fr-combat-006"></a>

### FR-COMBAT-006 — Attack animation is synchronized with the game action

**Behavior and acceptance.** Starting a melee/ranged/throwing action links the pose, direction, duration and the moment of the shot/blow to the result of the action; damage must not be dealt twice because of a repeated frame, and an outcome must not be skipped on a mode change. Acceptance checks hit/miss and the action frame for different weapon speeds and on interruption/incapacitation.

**Original functions and data.** Creating the attack goal (0x434AE0, 0x434B00) turns it into a throw under default-throw and checks that an attack is possible; the start of the attack animation (0x432700) spends AP and computes the pause from the critter's speed and the weapon speed ([RT8](../research/ledger/combat.md#real-time-combat-arc-combat-004)), and the update (0x432990) ends the animation on petrification, paralysis or stun and marks the action frame ([RT9](../research/ledger/combat.md#real-time-combat-arc-combat-004)); the attack goal tables match the ones read ([RT11](../research/ledger/combat.md#real-time-combat-arc-combat-004)). Needs actor/weapon ART events and FPS. The multiplayer modification of the animation and one weapon FX ID were not read. **Steamwoven:** only limited diagnostic playback, without combat actions and action events ([status][status]).

<a id="fr-combat-007"></a>

### FR-COMBAT-007 — Projectiles, throws and ammunition are resolved by weapon type

**Behavior and acceptance.** A ranged action selects a trajectory/projectile and accounts for the hit, a blocking actor/object, range and a miss; a thrown item can be lost or returned according to its type; a weapon with ammunition checks and spends it before the shot. Acceptance compares the projectile path, the blocker, ammo consumption and the miss result for a firing, a throwing and a returning variant.

**Original functions and data.** Ranged attack processing (0x4B39B0) sets the missile ART, the miss point offset and a double arrow for the corresponding training, and creates a projectile; projectile creation and flight resolution (0x4B24F0, 0x4B2650, 0x4B2690, 0x4B2870) handle flight, obstacles and the hit, weapon loudness (0x4B2810) the noise of the shot; ammunition consumption (0x4B65D0) checks type/count/consumption; a throw (0x4B3C00) creates a thrown item. The basis is a reading of the original's functions at the given addresses, level F0 (not checked in the exe), if there is no row in the verification ledger. The inventory lifecycle and item ammunition belong to the items section. **Steamwoven:** partial — the [hero ranged attack](../world/hero-attack-runtime.md#ranged-hero-attack-arc-combat-015) with a bow and firearms (ARC-COMBAT-015): range, line of fire, ammo consumption, a shot at a wall; projectile flight (ARC-COMBAT-016); the [throw of a throwing dagger](../world/hero-attack-runtime.md#thrown-weapons-arc-combat-017) (ARC-COMBAT-017): the weapon leaves the hand, a check with the Throwing skill with the throw range, the item lands where the projectile ended; a returning weapon, a second projectile and spread are not implemented ([status][status]).

<a id="fr-combat-008"></a>

### FR-COMBAT-008 — Damage is composed by type, resistance and protection

**Behavior and acceptance.** Each attack computes the normal/poison/electrical/fire/fatigue components, applies resistances and immunities, hit location/armor, weapon flags and the chosen difficulty; one call does not mix damage and fatigue. Permitted strikes on destructible portals/containers use the material and HP, and then invoke the bust/trap path. Acceptance compares the same attack against different resistances, armor slots and materials.

**Original functions and data.** Damage calculation (0x4B6680), resistance application (0x4B6860) and damage dealing (0x4B4390) compute the types/resistances, immunity and the consequences for the critter; armor selection by hit location and the portal/container damage branch are in damage dealing (0x4B4390). A critter's resistance is the sum of the base, worn armor, a poison bonus and effects ([EQ8](../research/ledger/items.md#equipment-arc-world-028)). Branches A1–A10 were checked in the exe by task ARC-COMBAT-001 ([original check](../research/combat-core-evidence.md#original-executable-check-arc-combat-001)). Armor and item stats are shared with the items section. **Steamwoven:** partial — the [attack resolver](../world/attack-resolver-runtime.md) (ARC-COMBAT-001) computes a damage packet by type with immunities, resistances, difficulty, the critical multiplier and the portal/container material; application to HP/Fatigue, armor wear by hit location and bust/trap are not implemented ([status][status]). Spell damage goes through the same step without an attack roll (`AttackResolver.DamageStep`, [ARC-MAGIC-012](../world/spell-damage-heal.md), DH5–DH7): resistances, full damage by current HP, the difficulty share (MG35), the caster's weapon in context (MG36).

<a id="fr-combat-009"></a>

### FR-COMBAT-009 — Critical hits and misses have consequences

**Behavior and acceptance.** A critical outcome uses the weapon hit/miss profile and the target body type for possible bonus damage, stun/knockdown/knockout, blind/cripple/scar, drop/damage/break gear or loss of ammo; the permitted effects are limited by the kind of target. Acceptance feeds scenarios on different body/chart combinations and confirms that the result and the affected object match the chosen table.

**Original functions and data.** Critical hit processing (0x4B5F40) and critical miss processing (0x4B6410) parse the hit and miss tables by hit, miss and body profile categories; the basis is a reading of the original's functions at the given addresses, level F0 (not checked in the exe), if there is no row in the verification ledger. Needs weapon/prototype chart fields, the target body type and RNG. **Steamwoven:** no critical resolver or profile data ([status][status]). Reading the functions does not replace checking individual values/probabilities in the original game data.

<a id="fr-combat-010"></a>

### FR-COMBAT-010 — Fatigue, knockout and death stay different outcomes

**Behavior and acceptance.** HP-death and fatigue-based unconsciousness are modeled separately: Fatigue can knock out without death, and fatigue recovery lets the character get up; stun, paralysis, stone, poison, blind/cripple and other statuses affect only explicitly assigned actions. In the original, the continuation of an automatic attack stops on the `OCF_NON_LETHAL_COMBAT` flag ([RT7](../research/ledger/combat.md#real-time-combat-arc-combat-004)), but this flag alone does not prove a conversion of damage into knockout or nonlethal behavior of a manual attack. Acceptance distinguishes a knockout from HP≤0, the auto-attack guard and lethal/manual attack paths.

**Original functions and data.** The unconsciousness check (0x45D800) uses a fatigue threshold with an exception for undead, and the death check (0x45D8D0) uses HP; setting fatigue damage (0x45D5A0) clamps a negative value to zero and initiates demaintain and knockdown, and the recovery event (0x45E610) can bring the actor back to consciousness. Healing (0x4B58C0) covers ordinary healing, clearing injuries/states and gated resurrect/reanimate (critter states (0x45D5A0, 0x45D670, 0x45D700, 0x45D730, 0x45D790, 0x45D800, 0x45D870, 0x45D8D0), recovery (0x45E610), combat states (0x4B4390), healing/resurrection (0x4B58C0)); setting fatigue damage and the maximum fatigue were checked in the exe ([EF7](../research/ledger/character.md#durable-effects-arc-world-029), [EF8](../research/ledger/character.md#durable-effects-arc-world-029)), the fatigue recovery step (0x45E610) by task ARC-COMBAT-002 ([contract](../world/life-state-runtime.md)). The continuation of an automatic attack (0x429760) checks `OCF_NON_LETHAL_COMBAT` ([RT7](../research/ledger/combat.md#real-time-combat-arc-combat-004)), while the damage-dealing branches reviewed (0x4B4390) do not use it as a damage conversion. **Steamwoven:** partial, [ARC-COMBAT-002](../world/life-state-runtime.md): the life rules (knockout without death, death at HP ≤ 0, undead without loss of consciousness, fatigue recovery checked against the original exe, healing and resurrection) and their state in the session; timers, the round, the viewer and the auto-attack check are not connected ([status][status]). In the viewer the hero falls unconscious, lies without a click on the world and gets up after recovery ([ARC-COMBAT-021](../world/hero-attack-runtime.md#hero-knockdown-arc-combat-021), UC1–UC5: knockdown 0x435A90, getting up 0x434DE0 from recovery 0x45E610, click 0x54ED30). The resurrection script (point 0x12) is a port of the healing rules: the original runs it before checking the flags, and a refusal by any of them stops the resurrection ([ARC-MAGIC-012](../world/spell-damage-heal.md), DH10).

<a id="fr-combat-011"></a>

### FR-COMBAT-011 — The combat interface shows the mode, AP and the queue

**Behavior and acceptance.** During turn-based combat the player sees the available/reserved AP, the current participant, the end of the turn and an action refusal; actions from the keyboard and from buttons invoke the same turn resolver. Acceptance compares the visible cost with the actual AP after an attack, a move and a spell/item action, and the AI→PC transition does not mask whose turn is awaited.

**Original functions and data.** The combat interface draws the AP indicator and the NPC-turn cue and handles the button/E for the transition to the next participant (0x4B7690); interface panel initialization (0x57C6E0) links the UI callbacks with spell maintenance, skill, fatigue/health and tech UI (AP lamps (0x56F430), the AP bar with the panel hidden (0x56EFA0), another participant's turn (0x56F660, 0x56F840), the end-of-turn button and E (0x56F9B0, [CT5](../research/ledger/combat.md#turn-based-host-arc-combat-020)), callback boundary (0x57C6E0)). Checked in the exe (ARC-UI-017, F2): 20 AP lamps show the remainder after the hovered action, its cost and the uncovered part (updated by every AP check and deduction), and on another participant's turn three lights run along them; the combat interface shows neither a queue nor the name of whoever is acting ([CH1–CH6](../research/ledger/ui-audio.md#combat-hud-arc-ui-017), [plan](../research/combat-hud.md)). A list of the round's participants with the current one marked is a Steamwoven addition approved by the owner on 2026-10-28: it is shown in the Steamwoven rules mode and hidden in Original. The linking of the panel callbacks (0x57C6E0) stays at level F0 (not checked in the exe). **Steamwoven:** the production gameplay UI and combat UI are absent; the synthetic display lab does not prove their coverage ([status][status]).

<a id="fr-combat-012"></a>

### FR-COMBAT-012 — Auto-attack and weapon selection follow the mode and settings

**Behavior and acceptance.** The player can switch on/off the automatic repetition of a permitted attack in real-time and a separate setting for automatic weapon selection. Auto-attack stops for an invalid target, in turn-based mode and in an explicitly forbidding state; a manual attack stays an independent action. Acceptance compares the on/off setting, target validity, active turn-based mode and the nonlethal flag, then checks the weapon switch after a suitable weapon is exhausted or lost.

**Original functions and data.** Reading auto attack (0x4B8230) returns false in turn-based combat ([RT5](../research/ledger/combat.md#real-time-combat-arc-combat-004)); the continuation of the attack animation checks PC/combat mode/living target/nonlethal flag/boomerang ([RT7](../research/ledger/combat.md#real-time-combat-arc-combat-004)). The PC weapon change (0x4B83E0) calls best-weapon selection (0x465170) if auto-switch is on; the Options UI connects both settings (auto-attack continuation (0x429760), settings and switch (0x4B8230, 0x4B8280, 0x4B82E0, 0x4B8300, 0x4B8330, 0x4B8380, 0x4B83E0), Options UI (0x589710, 0x589740)). Needs the current target, combat mode and equipped items. **Steamwoven:** the attack-repeat rule and the setting (on by default, as in the original, [RT6](../research/ledger/combat.md#real-time-combat-arc-combat-004)) exist as pure functions (ARC-COMBAT-004, [contract](../world/real-time-combat-runtime.md)); there are no hero attacks in the viewer and no weapon auto-switch; auto-weapon equipment details belong to the item report.

<a id="fr-combat-013"></a>

### FR-COMBAT-013 — Kill log and injury history

**Behavior and acceptance.** The player sees the accumulated kill statistics and the records of injuries received, including the linked entity/state in a supported profile. This data belongs to the game state and survives its own save/load; it is not derived from current animations or the number of visible bodies. Acceptance: a kill/event is not counted twice, ownership by the PC/leader is set by an explicit rule, and an injury record and its active state are displayed consistently. The formulas, the limits of recording and the attribution in the original game need a separate check.

**Original functions and boundaries.** Kill recording (0x4F5330) updates counters/notable-enemy records; the NPC death notification (0x45DA20) calls it for a PC killer or a PC leader (writer/getter (0x4F5330, 0x4F5590), death caller (0x45DA20)). The tab reads kill/injury data and formats the records (UI (0x53FBB0), formatting (0x5405C0)). Combat calls injury recording (critical caller (0x4B4390)). The guard condition of injury recording (0x4F5690), read in the exe (ARC-UI-097, [LB22](../research/ledger/ui-audio.md#logbook-arc-ui-008)): it exits if the attacker is empty, the victim is not a PC (type 15) or the attacker is not a PC/NPC; otherwise the cause is 30286 for self-damage, 30287 for another PC, otherwise the description of the attacker as the victim knows it (0x45F970). The earlier F0 reading "exits for a critter" was a reconstruction error, not a bug of the game.

**Dependencies and coverage.** Combat outcome, character injury state, journal UI and persistence; FR-SOCIAL-008 remains the owner of quest/social records. Steamwoven: the journal and the tab exist (ARC-UI-082), attack steps write the hero's kills and wounds (ARC-UI-097); not covered are damage without an attacker (spells, burning, traps, scripts) and the veto of the death script. The checks above are proposed, not performed.

### FR-MAGIC-001 — Spells and technological effects have loadable definitions

**Behavior and acceptance.** An ability definition reports the type (spell/tech), the college/degree association, the permitted action, target parameters, cost, duration/maintenance, save/resistance and effect components. The UI can show a definition from the connected data without replacing unknown fields with an invented card. Acceptance compares several actual data records by fields and checks an explicit status for unknown/incorrect components; the list of spell names is not specified here.

**Original functions and data.** Magic-tech initialization (0x44EF50) and record parsing (0x44FE30, 0x44FFA0) read `Rules\magictech.mes`, `mes\spell.mes`, `Rules\SpellEyeCandy.mes` and `Rules\spelllist.mes`, assembling records and effect components into a common typed runtime record (loader (0x44EF50), records/components (0x44FE30, 0x44FFA0, 0x450090, 0x4501D0)); the basis is a reading of the original's functions at the given addresses, level F0 (not checked in the exe), if there is no row in the verification ledger. **Steamwoven:** there is no magic-tech data model, invocation runtime or in-game spell/tech view ([status][status]); acceptance of the original records was not run.

<a id="fr-magic-002"></a>

### FR-MAGIC-002 — The spell book and prerequisites follow the college sequence

**Behavior and acceptance.** For learning a spell the UI shows the college/level and permits only the valid next link under the required level and attribute conditions; removing a spell does not break the sequence already learned. The tech degree and attribute thresholds are checked through a separate character contract. Acceptance checks the first and the next rank, the blocking of a skipped rank, an insufficient prerequisite and the removal of the last known spell.

**Original functions and data.** Learning a spell (0x4B1790) requires the next college rank, character level 1, 1, 5, 10, 15 by rank, intelligence and willpower ([AL6](../research/ledger/character.md#auto-level-schemes-arc-world-036)); removal (0x4B19B0) works only for the last college level. The spell interface (0x57C540, 0x57C670) links this with points and the UI (spell learning/removal (0x4B1750, 0x4B1760, 0x4B1770, 0x4B1790, 0x4B1950, 0x4B19B0), UI callers (0x57C540, 0x57C670)). The minimum intelligence (0x4502E0) in the original returns the constant 5 ([LM5](../research/ledger/magic.md#magic-arc-res-043-load-map-arc-res-040)), and the minimum willpower (0x4502F0) reads the `IQ:` field ([LM6](../research/ledger/magic.md#magic-arc-res-043-load-map-arc-res-040)), so this branch cannot be taken for the full prerequisite table (getter (0x4502E0, 0x4502F0)). **Steamwoven:** partial, [ARC-MAGIC-004](../world/spell-book-runtime.md): a single spell book rule (learning the next rank with intelligence, level and willpower checks, forced learning, removal of only the highest rank, mastery once at five ranks, a recheck of the ranks when intelligence or willpower drops), ±1 Magick point per rank, mastery is kept for the hero and NPCs (schema 25), NPC books from the creature fields of any map; learning and removal go through the shared points path with the editor floor (checked against the exe, [SB1–SB11](../research/ledger/magic.md#spell-book-arc-magic-004), learning — oracle F3); the character sheet's spells page in the viewer ([ARC-MAGIC-008](../rendering/terrain-viewer.md#spells-page-arc-magic-008)): colleges, ranks and hints as in the original, `+` and `−` through the same points path, a refusal names the check that failed (checked against the exe, [SG1–SG8](../research/ledger/magic.md#spell-page-arc-magic-008)).

<a id="fr-magic-003"></a>

### FR-MAGIC-003 — The target of a magical action is chosen by the definition's parameters

**Behavior and acceptance.** A cast can require oneself, an object, a group of objects or a tile/radius, checks range and line of sight, forbids an invalid target and keeps it only after a successful invocation. For an NPC the fallback to a valid tile must not change the player's rules without an explicit basis. Acceptance covers self, a valid object, blocked LOS, a target that is too far and the tile/radius case.

**Original functions and data.** The invocation check (0x4564E0) checks turn, range, LOS, target flags and the validity of the invocation; the LOS check (0x456BC0) accounts for blockers/shoot-through. Activation and application in the spell UI put the player into targeting and send the invocation; the target-list builder (0x4F40B0) supplies the candidates (invocation validation (0x4564E0), LOS (0x456BC0), spell UI (0x57BC70, 0x57BFF0, 0x57C040, 0x57C080, 0x57C0B0, 0x57C0E0, 0x57C110)). ### FR-MAGIC-004 — Casting a spell or a tech effect spends the specified resource

**Behavior and acceptance.** An ability checks its cost before the action, deducts the resource only once on a successful phase and handles a shortage without partial damage to the state.
