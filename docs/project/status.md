## Not implemented

There is no integrated playable world with an actor, movement, obstacle handling, interaction and dialog. Original-world navigation/collision, full SCR/quest execution, inventory, combat, persistence/save-load and world transitions remain future work. The synthetic gamepad lab’s movement and obstacle checks do not implement those original-world systems. The integrated WORLD candidate is a labelled finite diagnostic, not an integrated playable game or a claim of original-runtime parity.

The [vision](vision.md) records accepted product direction, including controller behavior. The [roadmap](roadmap.md) describes dependencies and evidence gates; active tasks and acceptance contracts remain on the Hermes board. Do not infer readiness from a viewer, synthetic lab, or code presence alone.
