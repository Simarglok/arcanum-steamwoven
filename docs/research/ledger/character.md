[`GaitTiming`](../../../src/Arcanum.World/ActorGait.cs) and the [hero body](../../rendering/actor-visual-contract.md#hero-body-arc-ui-039)
(FR-RUNTIME-004, FR-CHAR-003), on top of NW2, NW3, SH-04, N1 and research L6/L7. Question: which critter body the PC is drawn
with and how fast it walks and runs. Before this task every hero was drawn with the fixed human woman (a playtest found an elf man
drawn as a woman). Read on 2026-10-28 with `llvm-objdump` by the implementing task (F1); the art counts with the opt-in test
`HeroBodyArtTests.EveryCreatableBodyLoadsFromTheInstalledArt`.

| ID | Function (address) | Original 1.0.7.4 | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HB1 | race and gender set (0x441C70, NW3), race table (0x5C5170, NW2), pre-made prototypes (SH-04) | The PC's art id carries the race table's body type (bits 24..26: human, half-elf and half-orc 0, dwarf 1, gnome and halfling 2, half-ogre 3, elf 4) and the gender (bit 27, 1 a man); the twelve pre-made prototypes carry the same ids. The critter name rule draws an elf woman with the human body (N1). So the elf man is `EFM`, the halfling and gnome men `GHM`, the dwarf man `DFM`, the half-ogre man `HGM`, the human, half-elf and half-orc men `HMM` and every woman `HMF`. | Original; technical bug fixed in both modes (no setting): the viewer drew `HMF` for everyone |
| HB2 | walk/run rate (0x437990), art body (0x503EA0), art class (0x502700) | For a critter art id (class 2) whose body is 1 or 2 (dwarf, gnome, halfling) the rate adds 4 to the low, middle and high values of the walk and run rates (L6); it asks the art id, not the race. The rate is also the frame time of the walk or run animation (L6), so a body whose ART header says another rate (the `GHM` walk and run say 12) still plays at the rate. | Original; the viewer's walk and run animations now play at the gait rate (equal to the human's header rate at Speed 8, so the human is unchanged) |
| HB3 | stride (research L7) | The stride is the ART's frame offsets.
