**Implementation** (ARC-MAGIC-028): `WorldSector.AptitudeAdjustment()` (AQ2, AQ3) and `WorldSector.TileArt` (AQ6) in `Arcanum.Data`; `TileSoundTable.NameFlags`, `MageGround` and `IsOutdoor` (AQ7);
`CharacterStatContext` gains `Background`, `Hour`, `OutdoorTile` and `NatureTile`, and `CharacterStatRules.Situational` adds the ±4 to magick points (AQ6). The caller that builds the context still has to read the sector
of the critter's location and pass the four facts; the session and viewer callers pass 0 and no background until that wiring slice.

**Wiring** (ARC-MAGIC-031, the hero only): `AreaSectorRecord.AptitudeAdjustment` carries the sector file's second metadata word into the area snapshot (the same word as `WorldSector.AptitudeAdjustment()`; 0 for an older file); `AreaHeroGround` reads, from the shown window's snapshot at the hero's committed cell, the adjustment of the cell's sector (cell >> 6) and the tile art's outdoor and nature facts (`TileSoundTable.MageGround`; the nature flag needs the tile-name table, outdoor does not); `GameSessionOwner.HeroGround` (set by the viewer wherever it takes a session, not saved) hands them to `GameSessionOwner.HeroStatContext(poison, crippledLegs)`, which adds the hero's background block (field 0x101) and the game hour of the session clock (0..23). The hero's sheet, stats page, inventory view, carry facts, equipment aptitude, the spell host's effective stats and the healer support facts read that context. A cell the window holds no sector or tile for answers the neutral ground (the original answers the same where its sector cannot be locked). **Not wired:** NPCs (their sector adjustment and tile facts: NPC backgrounds are 0 and they have no mage background, but an NPC's aptitude does follow its sector in the original), the attack paths that read a copied stat owner (the weapon's aptitude in a hero attack among them), and backgrounds 66–68 (AQ8). The item-script trial reads the same context as the hero's own aptitude. The carry level is recomputed on an inventory change only, so a carry level that depends on aptitude can lag a sector or hour change until the next change of the pack.
