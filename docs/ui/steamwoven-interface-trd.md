## Terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Composition | The one centred 4:3 block of the Steamwoven interface: the original 800 × 600 units, top strip 41, window zone (or the world) 400, bottom strip 159. |
| Master | A part as authored for the master composition of 2880 × 2160 px, which is the 3840 × 2160 reference screen without its 480 px side areas (the sides show the world or black). Measured in master pixels (px). |
| Step | One of the four baked sets: ×1, ×2/3, ×1/2, ×1/3 of the masters. |
| `s` | Device pixels per UI unit for the current window (fractional). |
| `f` | Device pixels per master pixel for the current window: `f = s / 3.6`. |
| UI unit | One pixel of the original 800 × 600 layout. In the master, 1 unit = 3.6 px. |
| Strip | The top strip (800 × 41 units) or the bottom strip (800 × 159 units) of the composition. |
| Window zone | The 800 × 400 units between the strips: the world on the play screen, a menu window when one is open. |
| Sides | The parts of the window left and right of the composition (above and below it when the window is narrower than 4:3). They show the world or black, never wood. |
| Family | A group of menus whose backgrounds share one material and so one window-background set. |
| Original look / Steamwoven look | The interface with the HD option off / on ([ADR](steamwoven-interface-adr.md#context)). Both use the one composition ([Original look in the composition](#original-look-in-the-composition)); they differ in art, in what the sides show and in the world's scale. |

## Screen frame and scale

- **SWI-LAY-1** The Steamwoven layout is the composition scaled to the window. For a window of W × H device pixels,
  `s = min(W / 800, H / 600)` (fractional) and `f = s / 3.6 = min(W / 2880, H / 2160)`. At the 3840 × 2160 reference `s` = 3.6, `f` = 1 and the composition is the master composition, 2880 × 2160 px.
- **SWI-LAY-2** Every aspect is supported by scaling the one centred composition; there is no other layout. 4:3 is native: the composition fills the window and there are no sides. A wider window (16:10, 16:9, 21:9, 32:9)
  is height-limited: the composition has the window's full height and the sides are left and right of it. A narrower window (5:4 and below) is width-limited: the composition has the window's full width, centred, with black above and below.
- **SWI-LAY-3** The composition is `800 · s` × `600 · s` px, centred; its left, right, top and bottom edges are each the `round` of their own exact position (halves up), so its device size is the difference of two rounded edges (2560 × 1600: 213 and 2347, a width of 2134) and the sides are `round` of their own exact edge too. The sides show **the world** on the play screen and in windows opened over it (the world is drawn to the window's edges, not
  cropped to the composition, and is dimmed and desaturated while a window is open), and **black** beside full-screen menus and two-strip menus; the strip above and below a width-limited composition is always black. Nothing is drawn as wood in a gap: there are no gap rectangles, no frame bars at 16:10 and wider, and no
  pillarbox at 21:9 and 32:9.
- **SWI-LAY-4** The baked step is the smallest of ×1, ×2/3, ×1/2, ×1/3 that is ≥ `f`. Above ×1 (`f` > 1: 5K screens, 3840 × 2400) the ×1
  set is scaled up (a recorded limit, see [open questions](#open-questions)). Below ×1/3 the ×1/3 set is scaled down
  to `f` = 0.25; below 0.25 (a window narrower than 720 px at 4:3) the Steamwoven interface is not offered and the Original look, which has its own composition scale (SWI-ORG-1), is used.
- **SWI-LAY-5** The run-time scale is `f / step`. `f`, the step choice and the test for an exact step are computed
  exactly (integers or rationals; for a width-limited window `f` is exactly the step 2/3 when `3 · W = 2 · 2880` and is at most 2/3 when `3 · W ≤ 2 · 2880`), never with floating-point equality. When it is exactly 1 the parts are drawn at whole device pixels with
  no filtering; otherwise with linear filtering from the baked set.
- **SWI-LAY-6** W × H are device (backing) pixels, including the macOS backing scale.
- **SWI-LAY-7** Every kit master length and offset is a multiple of 6 px, so it is a whole number of pixels at every step
  (6 px = 4, 3 and 2 px); the strip heights are the open exception ([open questions](#open-questions)). A device position is `round(master position · f)`, computed per edge (not per size), so
  neighbouring parts share their edges; halves round up. The two strips are the exception: each is `round(units · s)` high from the composition's own top or bottom edge (41 and 159 units) and the window zone is what is left
  between them, so a strip never loses a pixel to a neighbour's rounding.

## Play screen layout

- **SWI-LAY-10** The composition's three bands are the original's: top strip 41 units, window zone 400, bottom strip 159 (master 147.6, 1440 and 572.4 px, which are off the 6 px grid: [open question](#open-questions); device heights by SWI-LAY-7). Only this pair (k = 1) exists: the thin pair of
  120 / 456 master px (owner decision 2026-11-17, [#2453](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2453)) and `k` = 0.8 are retired, because the strips and an open window together are exactly 4:3 at the original proportions and the composition fills the height.
- **SWI-LAY-11** The HUD is the original layout at `s`: every control sits at its original unit position and size × `s`, the HUD text (message window, clock, counters) is drawn at the same scale, and nothing is re-laid by a layout
  model. The strips are composition-wide (800 units). Elements that stand at an edge over the world (the spell pane, the combat list, text bubbles and floaters) are placed against the window's edges, not against the composition; the follower group has its own rule, SWI-LAY-16.
- **SWI-LAY-12** A strip is one image (the strip art) plus hardware layers over it (sockets, rings, vial housings, the message window frame, rails, buttons; hardware is a separate layer, not baked into the strip). There is **no board and no sides**:
  beside the composition the world shows. Each strip's outer ends carry an **end cap** (a brass edge and a soft shadow on the world, kit kind `cap`), so the strip does not end in a bare cut.
- **SWI-LAY-13** Hit areas are the rectangles (or circles) of the drawn parts from the layout, not the original's
  pixel masks.
- **SWI-LAY-14** With no menu open the window zone is the world view, and the world is visible at the sides to the window's edges (at the player's whole zoom step, SWI-LAY-17). The out-of-sight rules follow the owner decision
  of 2026-11-07 ([decisions](../architecture/decisions.md), "Out-of-sight rules and the view": Steamwoven tests the
  drawn view); how wide the drawn world may be on an ultrawide window is an [open question](#open-questions).
- **SWI-LAY-15** No text is baked into any part; all text is drawn with fonts
  ([#2462](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2462)): the original's fonts (HD frames in HD), sizes per step in the
  [font note](steamwoven-interface-fonts.md).
- **SWI-LAY-16** The follower group moves as one: the portraits, the scroll arrows and the show/hide button (FP1: the button 67 × 13 units at (0, 428), the arrows at (0, 415) and (33, 415), the portraits at x 11, y 50 + 62 per slot) stay **inside the composition** on their original places, counted from the composition's left edge and top, at its scale `s`;
  the button stands on the top left corner of the bottom strip, as in the original, at every window size, and the group is identical to the original at 4:3. **Owner decision 2026-11-20 ([#2925](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2925)):** no group part is carried to the window's edge and the button has no backing of its own (a first plan, the group at the window's left edge on a small brass backing, was built, compared in a composite and dropped). **As built (#2925):** `AreaFollowerPortraits` hangs from the strips' screen (`AreaGameBarPanel.TopScreen`) in the column mode as in the full-width layout; the Steamwoven bar smoke checks every part's place, the button's corner and the group's left edge at each size.

- **SWI-LAY-17** **World zoom (owner decision 2026-11-19, [#2937](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2937)).** The world zoom stays a **player setting for now**: the existing in-game zoom option is kept and not changed by this layout. The **working reference look is the composition's scale `s`** (option B of the issue; the default is not fixed, see below): the world scales with the interface, the play view inside the window zone shows the same area as the original (800 × 400 units), the sides add only what lies beyond it, and sprites match the interface's size. Not chosen as the reference: world pixels at 1× (A, much more world on a large screen, small sprites). The HD sprites question stays closed (world frames 1×, decision 2026-11-15). The Original look draws its world at `s` only inside the composition (SWI-ORG-3). **Decided 2026-11-20 (option C, [#2937](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2937), built by [#3059](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/3059)):** in the Steamwoven look the player's zoom is a **whole step** 1×, 2×, 3× or 4× (device pixels per world pixel), capped by `s` = min(width / 800, height / 600): **the rule (confirmed by the owner 2026-11-21: the nearest whole step, above `s` by at most a quarter step):** a step is offered while it is at most `s` + `Tolerance`, the tolerance a constant of ¼ (`SteamwovenWorldZoom.Tolerance`), so the highest step is floor(`s` + ¼), 4× needs `s` ≥ 3.75 and a window under `s` = 1.75 offers 1× only. The default (the `world zoom` setting `auto`) is the highest step offered: 1920 × 1080 (`s` = 1.8) 2×, 2560 × 1440 (2.4) 2×, 3840 × 2160 (3.6) 3×, 3024 × 1964 (3.27) 3×. A whole step keeps the 1× world art crisp (the nearest rule draws a texel as whole pixels). The setting is the `world zoom` line of the game settings (`auto` or 1–4), the launcher row "World zoom (generated)" (steps above the window's cap greyed) and the palette's zoom actions (nearest step); a resize that lowers the cap lowers the shown step, and the step returns when the window grows again. Hit testing, edge scroll, the floaters and bubbles and the HUD overlays use the one canvas transform the camera drives, so they follow the step; the follower group (SWI-LAY-16) is HUD and does not move. **Accepted consequence:** the out-of-sight rules test the drawn view (SWI-LAY-14), so they follow the zoom: at a higher step the drawn view, and the reach of the rules, is smaller. Example, 1920 × 1080 at the default 2×: the window zone (400 units, 720 px) shows 360 world rows against the original's 400, and the width shows 960 world columns against 800 (the sides add what lies beyond the zone), and the rules' rectangle is the 960 × 540 the window draws. The Original look is unchanged, and the diagnostic WORLD mode keeps its free zoom.

## Prototype of the play screen (ARC-UI-2452)

> **Trimmed 2026-11-20 by [#2926](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2926).** The full-width prototype of 2026-11-16 to 2026-11-18 (the strips' boards and the 120 / 456 panel pair, the `k` = 0.8 stand-in scale, the side boards, the per-family backdrop pieces over the whole middle band, the full-screen menu backdrop over the whole frame) is removed from the code and from this page; the [composition](#screen-frame-and-scale) and [Window backgrounds and plates](#window-backgrounds-and-plates) replace it. What remains is what the column still uses.

Slice [#2452](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2452): the viewer lays the play screen out by the layout model with the **generated chrome** as the placeholder (and the fallback where the kit lacks a part, SWI-RUN-3). It is on while the HD option and the generated interface are both on (`AreaGameBarPanel.SteamwovenOn`); with the HD option off, or with the generated interface off, the Original look stands ([Original look in the composition](#original-look-in-the-composition)).

- **Scale.** The two strips keep the original's 800-unit layout and are drawn at the column's one fractional scale (`SteamwovenHud.Plan`: `Column.StripScale`, `3.6 · f` at k = 1, or frame height / 600 in the fitted column). The top strip stands on the bottom edge of the top band and the bottom strip hangs from the top edge of the bottom band; in the unfitted column (`column-wood`) a 41-unit strip is 147.6 · f px in a 150 · f band (a sliver of the strip's wood, the only board, shows above it) and a 159-unit strip is 572.4 · f px in a 570 · f band (its last 2.4 · f px lie beyond the frame's bottom edge); in the fitted column the strips and the bands coincide.
- **Menus.** `AreaGameBarPanel.TopInset` and `BottomInset` are the middle band's, so every window that docks between the strips docks in it.
- **Windows in the window zone (ARC-UI-2575).** Every menu window of the middle band takes its place from one hook, `AreaGameBarPanel.ZoneWindow` / `ZoneScreen` (`SteamwovenWindowZone`, Godot-free, unit-tested): the window is drawn at the column's unit scale (1.8 at 1920 × 1080, 2.4 at 2560 × 1440, 3.6 at 3840 × 2160) and its 800 × 400 body is the window zone of SWI-BDR-2. It covers the inventory, loot, barter (docked and undocked), character sheet (also the creation editor's screen), logbook, world map, schematics, reading, save/load and the shop strips; the pre-made browser and the new-character screen take the same hook (the screen views place the 800 × 600 screen so its body lies in the zone). The hook reads the window size alone, so the windows that hide the bars (loot, barter, reading, save/load) follow the same zone. With the HD option or the generated interface off the windows keep the Original look's place. The smoke `bar --env ARC_AREA_BAR_STEAMWOVEN=1` opens the inventory, sheet, logbook, map, schematics, the save list, the loot window of a container (See Contents), the barter window of the first NPC that can trade, a readable book, and the main menu's pre-made and new-character screens (ARC-UI-2614) at each size and checks each drawn body against the hook, saving `…-hud-steamwoven-VARIANT-WxH-WINDOW.png`.
- **One wood per family (ARC-UI-2576, ARC-UI-2643, ARC-UI-2763).** The wood of the unfitted column's gaps is chosen by the window on top of the interface mode stack (`SteamwovenBackdrops.FamilyOf`: an overlay such as the quantity picker keeps the window's own wood): BD-A for the inventory, loot, steal, barter, identify and repair windows, BD-B the character sheet, BD-C the logbook, BD-D the reading window, BD-E the world map, BD-F the schematics; the dialog and sleep views have none. A piece is generated wood sampled at the window's unit scale, its grain starting at the window art it continues; a two-part family (SWI-BDR-5) has two pieces that meet at the split (the inventory family splits at the zone's left edge plus 358 units, the logbook 165 and the reading window 132; the logbook's and the reading window's right half starts its own grain at the split, the inventory family's grain and wood are one). Each piece's wood is tuned to the tone of the window art it continues (`SteamwovenBackdrops.ToneArt`: the inventory 221 for both halves, the sheet 22, the logbook's side 264 and book 260, the reading side bar 494, the map frame 138, the schematic base 365). The fitted column has no gap and so no piece. The smoke (`SteamwovenBackdrops`, `CheckBackdrop`) checks the family, the pieces' rectangles and texels, and the seam ratio R of SWI-VER-5 (`SteamwovenSeam`: the difference of neighbouring pixels in a ±8 px band at the join over the one in a band beside it, inside the middle band; taken at the cleanest twentieth of the edge's rows or columns, because parts of the window that lie at its edge only raise it and a real seam shows on every line) at the top and bottom edge of the window zone (R ≤ 1.5; the bottom edge is printed only, because the windows' own look switch lies just below it). The split of a two-part window is a designed join of two arts, exempt from R. The barter, identify and repair and reading windows keep the bars (`GameBarActive`; the original hides them), so that every menu window of the middle band has its family.
- The combat list and the spell pane ([#2604](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2604)) take `AreaGameBarPanel.FreeRect`: the spell pane starts 12 px right of the frame's left edge (`FreeLeft`) and the combat list ends 8 px left of the frame's right edge; the text bubbles' placement clamp ([#2603](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2603)) takes `AreaGameBarPanel.FreeRect` too. The sleep and fate views keep their own whole scale, placed on the strips' screen (`AreaGameBarPanel.TopScreen`). The strips are not snapped to the 6 px grid and are sampled Linear.
- **Full-screen menus** ([#2577](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2577), now [Menus in the column](#menus-in-the-column-arc-ui-2891)). The main, single-player, new-game, in-play and options windows (the census' BD-H: the windows of `AreaMenuArtView`) are drawn as the plate fitted to the window's height, black beside it; hit testing is the control's own transform, so it follows the drawn plate (`SteamwovenFullScreenPlan.HitTest` is the same mapping as plain numbers, unit-tested). Credits and View Intro (a black fill) and the two-strip menus are not part of the plan of this slice.
- **Smoke.** `./arcanum smoke pr --stages bar --env ARC_AREA_BAR_STEAMWOVEN=1 --env ARC_HD_PACK=<pack> --env ARC_AREA_BAR_CAPTURE=<prefix>` checks the plan, the strips' places, the insets, the boards, the bars and the open-menu wood at the SWI-VER-6 sizes (`ARC_STEAMWOVEN_SIZES=WxH,...` to choose others) for the variants of `ARC_STEAMWOVEN_VARIANTS` (`column` = the fitted column, the default; `column-wood` = the unfitted one; `kit` = the kit runtime on a synthetic kit; `legacy` = the Original look for comparison ([#2672](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2672): the HD option off for that pass, Legacy art, no plan, the same sizes, inventory and window probes saved as `<prefix>-hud-steamwoven-legacy-WxH[-menu|-WINDOW].png`, with no layout checks). ### The Original look in the viewer (ARC-UI-2936)

Issue [#2936](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2936). The Godot-free part is `OriginalComposition` (`tools/terrain-viewer/Core`): the scale `s`, the whole factor `n = ceil(s)`, the virtual screen `800 n × 600 n`, the composition's rectangle (the rounding of `SteamwovenColumnMenu.Fit`), the black rectangles, the pointer mapping, the camera's zoom factor and the fixed out-of-sight window; `OriginalCompositionTests` carry them (16:9, 16:10, 21:9, 4:3, 5:4, narrower than 4:3).

- **Mechanism (SWI-ORG-1, SWI-ORG-4).** While the HD option is off, no launcher screen is open and the run is not an automated one, the viewer's window gets the content scale `Viewport`, aspect `Keep`, size `800 n × 600 n` (`TerrainViewerApp.SyncOriginalComposition`, each frame, so a resized window follows). The whole interface and the world are then laid out in that virtual screen by the existing whole-factor rules at `n` (the strips at `n`, the windows and menus fitted to a virtual screen of exactly 800 × 600 units at `n`, the follower group and bubbles at their places), Godot draws the picture centred, reduces it smoothly (bilinear) to `s` and leaves the rest black (SWI-ORG-2), and maps the pointer back (SWI-ORG-5). At a whole `s` (`n = s`) there is no reduction and the picture is the nearest-pixel one. The launcher, the Steamwoven look (HD option on) and automated runs keep the window's own pixels.
- **World (SWI-ORG-3).** The camera's zoom is the player's setting times `n / 2` (`TerrainCameraController.ZoomScale`): the setting's default 2 shows exactly 800 × 400 world pixels between the strips at every `s`, and the in-game zoom option keeps working from there ([#2937](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2937): the setting stays). In the Original look the generators' and the restock's out-of-sight test and `WindowSize` are the fixed 800 × 400 centred on the view's centre (`AreaGameWindow`).
- **Smoke.** The `legacy` pass of the bar smoke (`./arcanum smoke pr --stages bar --env ARC_AREA_BAR_STEAMWOVEN=1 --env ARC_HD_PACK=<pack> --env ARC_STEAMWOVEN_VARIANTS=legacy [--env ARC_STEAMWOVEN_LEGACY_SIZES=WxH,...] [--env ARC_STEAMWOVEN_LEGACY_BEFORE=1] --env ARC_AREA_BAR_CAPTURE=<prefix>`) lays the viewer out for each size as above (the window cannot always be the size of a 4K target, so the composition takes the target as its size) and checks the HD option off, the whole factor, the strips at `n` with the original's insets, `400 n` of play view between them, the camera's zoom and the 800 × 400 out-of-sight window, and at a whole `s` that the Legacy strips are drawn with nearest pixels (every `n × n` block uniform). The captures are the virtual screen reduced to `s` on black, composed in software (what the window shows); `ARC_STEAMWOVEN_LEGACY_BEFORE=1` also saves the old layout as `-hud-steamwoven-legacy-before-WxH` for the owner's before / after composite. Default sizes: 3840 × 2160, 2560 × 1440, 1920 × 1080, 2560 × 1600, 1280 × 800, 3440 × 1440, 1024 × 768, 1280 × 1024, and the whole scales 1600 × 1200 and 3200 × 1800 for the pixel check.
- **Play view and map edge.** The camera's focus stands at the middle of the 400-unit play view (`ViewShift`, the camera's offset), not at the virtual screen's middle, and the map-edge clamp takes the play view's height (`PlayHeight`), so the hero can reach the map's edge inside the play view. A press on the black sides (outside the virtual screen) is dropped before it reaches the world (SWI-ORG-5); a release is not. **Open:** that Godot's final scaling of the virtual screen to the window is smooth (not nearest) at a fractional `s` is assumed from the Viewport stretch mode and is not read from real window pixels (a capture of the real window is unavailable to automated runs); the captures are composed in software.

State overlays are `<plate id>_hover` and `<plate id>_pressed` at the plate's size. A part named otherwise is not found by the runtime and its button stays on the generated or pack art. The ring is not a button part (see the buttons bullet of the runtime section).
- **SWI-KIT-2** A master is a PNG, RGBA 8 bit, sRGB, straight alpha, with sides that are multiples of 6 px.
- **SWI-KIT-3** A part used in several windows (a reuse group of the census) is one part at one master size.
- **SWI-KIT-4** A part with transparency has a clean edge: no halo of the generator's background colour (checked by
  the fit tool, SWI-VER-3).
- **SWI-KIT-5** A button is plate + ring + glyph + state overlays. The states of one button size (idle, hover, pressed,

- **SWI-KIT-6** A gauge is glass, housing and liquid as layers; the fill level is drawn from the value, not from a frame
  sequence.
- **SWI-KIT-7** Order of production: the strips, end caps and window backgrounds, then buttons and glyphs, sheets, the schematic, gauges and
  decor; icons, portraits and cursors last (the owner: all interface art is redrawn).

- **SWI-GEN-1** Context images come first: whole-screen redraws of the key screens (HUD, inventory, character,
  logbook, schematic, one reading window, main menu) at the master composition size (2880 × 2160), so Codex sees what each part is for. Parts are
  then generated with a crop of the context image as reference.
- **SWI-GEN-2** The approved material sheet freezes the tones and textures; every later part is generated against it
  and colour-locked to it.
- **SWI-GEN-3** A part's references are the context crop and the material sheet, plus the original art of the part
  (crop, frame or screenshot): original art is given to Codex as input (owner decision 2026-11-16,
  [#2448](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2448)). Its prompt
  comes from the prompt library `tools/steamwoven_ui/prompts/` (text only).
- **SWI-GEN-4** A generation becomes a master only through the fit tool (size, grid, alpha clean-up, low-frequency
  colour lock, provenance record). **As built (ARC-DEV-2459):** `tools/steamwoven_ui/fit.py` ([toolchain section](../workflow/toolchain.md#steamwoven-kit-fit-arc-dev-2459)):
  crop and scale to the manifest size (area average down, Catmull-Rom up, premultiplied, linear light), real alpha or a keyed flat background, edge decontamination against the nearest opaque colour,
  alpha clean-up (levels and specks), the colour lock to the approved sheet (SWI-GEN-11) by material, and the record `provenance/fit/<part id>.json`; a master is written only when the gates of SWI-VER-3 hold.
  Needs of the pilot production ([#2950](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2950)): per part the batch names the sheet materials (`--material`) and, if the generator returns no alpha, the key; the batch PR then sets the part's `status` to `master` and its `provenance` to `fit:<part id>`.
- **SWI-GEN-5** The strips and the window backgrounds are made by **method A** (owner decision 2026-11-21,
  [#2458](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2458), from the comparison
  [#2457](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2457)): one Codex item per zone at the generator's size, upscaled to the zone;
  methods B (overlapping tiles) and C (seamless material plus macro image) are dropped. A gives the wooden surface with its relief and nothing else; its five
  conditions are those of the [owner pick](steamwoven-surface-method-study.md#owner-pick-and-conditions-2026-11-21): (1) text, counters, digits and fields are not part of the
  generated surface, the game draws them over it; (2) zones filled in play are taken by the original's mask, never from the generation; (3) metal parts come from the kit
  (pilot [#3058](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/3058)); (4) seams at the strip joins not worse than the original's (1.82 and 1.73), the target is at most 1.8
  (SWI-VER-5 below keeps its own gate); (5) wood tone: mean L\* and contrast (and chroma) fitted to the original by the wood mask, random dark specks removed, the original's rows of dots kept.
  Method note and numbers: [steamwoven-surface-method-study.md](steamwoven-surface-method-study.md).
- **SWI-GEN-6** Every generated image has a provenance record: generator and version, date, prompt id and the hash of
  its text, reference ids and hashes, who ran it. The batch runner (SWI-GEN-7) also records the Codex thread id, the pixel size, the
  outcome and the refusal text.
- **SWI-GEN-7** Codex image generation runs through the owner's subscription Codex CLI on the Mac (owner decision 2026-11-16,
  [#2449](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2449), changed 2026-11-19, [#3001](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/3001):
  agents may run it). No API and no key: the built-in `image_gen` tool of the logged-in CLI only. Handoff: the agent builds a batch inbox
  (`local/steamwoven-ui/inbox/<batch>/`: prompts, references, `batch.json`, `README.txt`), runs `tools/steamwoven_ui/codex_batch.py`, which writes
  `local/steamwoven-ui/outbox/<batch>/<id>.png` with a provenance record per image, reports the files, sizes and refusals to the owner, and then runs the fit tool.
  The owner can still fill the outbox by hand and run `prompt_library.py provenance`.
- **SWI-GEN-8** The result of an item is accepted only as a PNG at the asked aspect (within 1 %); the generator keeps a budget of about 1.57 MP per
  image, so a master size above it is reached by the fit tool, never by an upscale inside the generation session. A refusal or a failure is
  logged with Codex's own text; the prompt is never reworded automatically. **Extreme aspects** (decision of ARC-UI-3083,
  [#3083](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/3083), FR-UI-007): for the strips (19.5:1, 5:1) the generator returns a 2103 × 748 canvas with the piece drawn
  at the right proportion between white or black bars. The cropped band is accepted as the result when the tool step (`codex_batch.py run --crop-band`, or `codex_batch.py crop`) produced it:
  the band's aspect is within 1 % of the asked one before the resize, the columns cut to land on it are at most 2 % of the band's width, the band is cut, never stretched, before the resize to the zone, and the provenance record
  keeps the crop section and the hash of the canvas Codex returned. A canvas with no bars, or a band that cannot reach the aspect, stays `wrong-aspect`. The resize of the band to the zone size
  is part of that step (Lanczos, an upscale of about x1.37 for the two strips); it is not an upscale inside the generation session.
- **SWI-GEN-9** A context image is a refinement of its ORIGINAL (owner decision 2026-11-20, [#2455](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2455)): the upscaled ORIGINAL is
  given to Codex as the one image to edit ("refine this image, raise its detail, do not change its colour or its shape"), and the redraw adds only resolution and surface texture: the same
  palette, brightness, contrast and shapes, no new ornament, shine or detail; brass is dull, dark and matte, glass slightly cloudy, the whole look dusty and muted. A tone check follows every
  generation (`tools/steamwoven_ui/tone_check.py`): both images are reduced to 800 × 600, converted to CIE L\*a\*b\* and blurred (box, 6 px), and compared per material (wood, brass, paper or
  parchment, iron) and for the whole over the pixels of the ORIGINAL that belong to a material; the layout masks of the content that the redraw leaves empty (text fields, portrait, schematic
  picture, items in slots, counters, the HUD world rows) are in `prompts/tone_masks.json`. Limits: |ΔL\*| ≤ 3, mean chroma within 12 %, contrast (std of L\*) within 12 % unless the std
  differs by less than 2 L\*, mean ΔE76 ≤ 6, 95th percentile ΔE76 ≤ 15. A region over a limit is redrawn (at most two redraws, then the owner decides); the region tone fit (`tone_check.py fit`:
  shift of L\* and scale of chroma inside one material to the ORIGINAL's blurred means, texture and shape kept) is the fallback for a material whose texture is right and tone is not.
- **SWI-GEN-10** The material sheet is cut from the approved, tone-fitted context images, not drawn (owner decision 2026-11-20, [#2455](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2455)): `tools/steamwoven_ui/cutouts.py cut` crops
  the rectangles of `prompts/cutouts.json` (800 × 600 layout coordinates, scaled by the source width) out of the contexts into `local/steamwoven-ui/material-sheet/cutouts/` with a provenance record each (source file and hash, layout and pixel rectangle, note); a cut-out with a side under 120 layout units also gets `<id>.context.png`, the surroundings (at least 240 × 180 layout units), which goes to Codex beside the small cut-out as its reference (owner decision 2026-11-20). A cut-out that no longer
  appears in the list is deleted by the next `cut`.
  The sheet is the sixteen cut-outs themselves (the twelve of the first sheet, plus, by the owner's order of 2026-11-20, the wood of the bottom strip cut from the gap between the sync and gear buttons, the two round brass button plates with their rim, key and gear, and the blue vial beside the red one; the red one is `vial-red`). The parchment cut-out is the schematic's with its printed grid; the schematic has none without it, the grid is a separate layer of the kit (its provenance note says so). The two round-button cut-outs sample the rim and the dome of the plate only; their symbols carry the original's dithering, are no sample of symbols and are redrawn clean as separate parts (owner note 2026-11-20). The sheet is the reference of tone and texture for the fit tool and for Codex; it is not tiled and needs no seamlessness (owner decision 2026-11-20). Paper and parchment belong to the window backgrounds (logbook, reading window, schematic) and are made whole by the window geometry, never by tiling; a parchment tile, if one is ever needed, has no grid (the grid is a separate layer, the Codex refusal of 2026-11-20 was about the grid). The one tile that exists is wood, made only as input for the strip method comparison ([#2457](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2457)): `tiles-inbox` (batch `style-anchors-tiles`) makes it from the cut-out as the image to edit; brass, glass, iron, cloth and the black fields stay cut-outs of the detail; no synthetic patches are added to any surface. `tile-check` compares a tile with the ORIGINAL
  material (mean L\* within 3, chroma within 12 %, the spread of brightness, the std of the blurred L\*, within 25 % of the original's unless it differs by under 1 L\*) and measures the seam ratio of the wrap-around join (the worse axis, at most 1.5). The tile is read at the context's scale (reduced by the context width / 800), a cut-out being a crop of a context; a tile is accepted only from the current inbox (its provenance prompt hash must match) and only as a square of at least 256 px.
- **SWI-GEN-11** The approved material sheet (owner approval 2026-11-20 in the [#2455](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2455) thread, after the three additions, four cut-outs; AC1 of [#2456](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2456)): `local/steamwoven-ui/material-sheet/sheet.png`, sha256 `4d5fb9c01fcec386d13a55a7deb133d99c86c93dd693dc23e9a8140ea555ab06`; its provenance record `local/steamwoven-ui/material-sheet/cutouts/cutouts.provenance.json`, sha256 `1f67f0190c3c0f5f82861ddbfcb49c2ec7bad04d07e76c3ce6ff66fab4b6ecf1` (sixteen cut-outs, each with its source context and hash). The files are local (never in git); a later change of the sheet is a new owner approval and a new hash here.

### Codex batch runner (ARC-DEV-3001)

[#3001](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/3001). `tools/steamwoven_ui/codex_batch.py` (standard library only; tests `python3 -B -m unittest tools.steamwoven_ui.test_codex_batch`, also in the batched `run_tests.py`)
runs one inbox batch and writes the outbox. It never writes into the inbox and never touches git. Its work folder is `local/steamwoven-ui/codex-work/<batch>/<run>/` (prompts sent, JSONL events, results and stderr per item).

- **`batch.json`** (written by `prompt_library.py inbox`, or by hand): `{"batch": NAME, "items": [...]}`; an item has `id` (plain name), `prompt_file` and `prompt_sha256` (required; the text is checked against it; files stay inside the inbox),
  `size` `[w, h]` or null and optional `aspect` `[w, h]` (the asked aspect is `aspect`, else `size`), `references` `[{"slot", "file", "sha256"}]` (files relative to the inbox, inside it, hash required and checked); `after` and other keys are ignored and `outbox_file` = `outbox/<batch>/<id>.png`.
- **Pre-check**: `codex --version`, `codex features list` shows `image_generation` on, `codex login status` is a subscription login (an API-key login stops the run). Any failure stops with the reason (exit 2) before anything is sent.
- **One session per batch**: the first item is `codex exec --skip-git-repo-check --sandbox workspace-write --enable image_generation --json -C <workdir> -o <result> [--image=REF ...] -` (prompt on stdin, with the session rules); the `thread_id` comes from the `thread.started` event and every later item is `codex exec resume <thread_id> ...` run from the same directory, strictly in order. Never `--last`, never `--ephemeral`, an argument list with no shell, `OPENAI_*` variables removed from the environment.
- **Per item**: exit code, the final JSON `{"ok", "path", "error"}`, the file `<id>.png` in the work folder, a PNG, the aspect within 1 %. Outcomes: `ok`, `refused` (ok false, with Codex's text), `failed`, `timeout` (`--timeout`, default 900 s, the process group is killed), `bad-result`, `missing-file`, `not-png`, `wrong-aspect`. The batch goes on after a failed item. Only an `ok` image is copied to the outbox, and an existing outbox file is kept unless `--overwrite`.
- **Provenance** `outbox/<batch>/<id>.provenance.json`: generator `codex-cli <version> image_gen`, date, batch, id, `prompt_sha256`, each reference with its sha256, `thread_id`, pixel size, asked size and aspect, outcome, refusal text, image sha256.
- **Commands**: `codex_batch.py check`; `codex_batch.py run BATCH --dry-run [--only ID,ID]` prints the commands and sends nothing; `codex_batch.py run BATCH [--only ID,ID] [--timeout S] [--overwrite] [--model M]`. Every `exec` and `exec resume` pins `-m gpt-6.1-sol -c model_reasoning_effort="medium"` (owner decision 2026-11-20, [#3043](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/3043)); `--model` overrides only with the owner's approval. Exit 0 all ok, 1 an item not ok, 2 pre-check or batch file failed.
- **Band crop** (ARC-UI-3083, `tools/steamwoven_ui/band_crop.py`, needs ImageMagick; only used with the option below): `run BATCH --crop-band` turns an item judged `wrong-aspect` whose canvas has bars above and below into `ok`
  (the file is cropped from the canvas in the work folder, a refusal or a failure by Codex is not cropped); `codex_batch.py crop IMAGE --size WxH --out OUT.png [--aspect W:H] [--fuzz P]` does the same for one file and
  writes `OUT.crop.json`. Steps: a 4 % fuzz trim of the colour at the canvas corner (each bar must be at least 5 % of the canvas height); a shave of 2 to 4 px at the top and the bottom (the light anti-aliasing line; the shave that
  brings the band closest to the asked aspect, 2 px at each side at least) and 2 px at a trimmed side edge; equal columns cut at both sides when the band is still wider than the asked aspect (at most 2 % of its width);
  a band aspect within 1 % of the asked one, else the band is refused with the reason in `detail`; a Lanczos resize to the item's `size` (8 bit RGBA PNG). Measured on the two canvases of #2457: 19.5:1 → band 2102 × 108 (bars 306/327 px, shave 4/3 px, 1 column cut, aspect +0.02 %),
  5:1 → band 2100 × 417 (bars 141/186 px, shave 2/2 px, 3 columns cut, +0.02 %). The provenance record adds `crop` (canvas, trim box, bars, shave, columns cut, band, aspect error, fuzz, filter, tool, `raw_sha256`, `cropped_sha256`), `raw_file` and `raw_pixel_size`.
- First real run (2026-11-19): a two-item synthetic text batch, both items `ok` in one resumed session; the tool returned 1448 × 1086 px (1.57 MP) for a 4:3 ask. Reference attachment (repeated `--image=PATH` before the `-` prompt, on `exec` and on `exec resume`) was checked once with two tiny synthetic images against the real CLI.
- The agent skill that drives it, `.claude/skills/steamwoven-codex-images/SKILL.md`, is narrow: only running ready interface image batches.

## Bake and runtime

- **SWI-RUN-1** The bake makes the four step sets from the masters, resampled in linear light with premultiplied
  alpha; every baked length is exactly the master length times the step; two runs give identical bytes; each set has
  a manifest with sizes and hashes. Tool and commands: [toolchain](../workflow/toolchain.md#steamwoven-kit-bake-arc-dev-2460) (`bake.py`, ARC-DEV-2460).
- **SWI-RUN-2** The runtime takes the step from SWI-LAY-4 and composes the layers of each part (strips with their end caps, hardware,
  plates, glyphs, states) inside the composition.
- **SWI-RUN-3** A missing part falls back to the generated chrome
  ([ARC-RES-122](../rendering/hd-interface-density.md)), so the HD option always draws a complete interface.
- **SWI-RUN-4** With the HD option off the Legacy art, its fonts and the original rules are unchanged; only the layout is the composition of SWI-ORG-1..5 ([#2936](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2936)). The 2026-11-16 requirement that the output stays byte-identical is superseded (owner decision 2026-11-19).
- **SWI-RUN-5** Only the background of the open window is resident (a ×1 window background is about 16.6 MB as raw RGBA, a full-screen plate about 24.9 MB); the
  strips, their end caps and the HUD hardware stay loaded.

### The kit runtime (ARC-UI-2461)

Issue [#2461](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2461). The Godot-free part is `SteamwovenKit` (the baked set: its manifest read, sizes checked against the master size times the step, files present, ids safe as file names; the folder resolver) and `SteamwovenKitComposer` with its table

- **Which set (SWI-LAY-4, SWI-LAY-5).** The step is the layout's (`SteamwovenLayout.Step`); one set is in use. A draw is **exact** when the baked pixels are the device pixels (the part's size equals its device rectangle); a view with only exact draws is sampled nearest, any other linear. The strip art is 150 and 570 master px high but the strips 148 and 572 device px at the reference size (SWI-LAY-7's open exception): its draws are scaled, linear, until the strip heights are fixed by [#2927](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2927).

Nothing is read with the HD option off, the generated interface off, or without the column layout.
- **Strips (SWI-LAY-12).** For each strip, the strip art `board.inttop` (art 185) or `board.intbotom` (184) in the column's strip rectangle, its hardware layers (`SteamwovenKitTable.Strips`, master positions from the strip's top left; empty until the hardware batch [#2481](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2481) places them) under the buttons and gauges, and its two end caps outside the strip's ends, as high as the strip, cut to the window (none at 4:3). A strip is drawn from the kit only when its art, its caps and every layer are in the set (and the art is 2880 px wide at the step); otherwise that strip keeps the pack's art and the generated caps and the other strip is not held back. The open menu's middle band keeps the generated cap, since the kit has no cap for it ([#3042](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/3042)).
- **Window backgrounds (SWI-BDR-2, -3, -5).** `SteamwovenKitTable.Windows` lists the pieces of a covered window left to right (master width, height 1440): inventory and the identify and repair windows (`backdrop.pdoll` 1290 + `backdrop.inventor` 1590), the loot, steal and See Contents windows ([#3032](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/3032): `backdrop.loot` 1290 + the same `backdrop.inventor`), character sheet, logbook (594 + 2286), world map and schematic. The pieces tile the zone exactly with edges rounded from their master position; a missing or wrong-sized piece makes the whole background missing and the generated backdrop stands. Layer 3 holds it under the window, with the lens holes as before. Only the open window's pieces stay resident (SWI-RUN-5). **Character sheet pages (ARC-UI-3035, census G06 to G10).** The sheet's four pages (skills 29, tech 30, spells 31, auto-level schemes 567; 235 × 325 units at (503, 63)) are panels laid over the character backdrop `backdrop.char_maint`: `SteamwovenKitTable.Sheets` maps each art to its kit sheet (`sheet.skills_window`, `sheet.disciplines_window`, `sheet.char_spells`, `sheet.scheme_rot`, 846 × 1170 master px). ## Provenance and storage

- **SWI-PRV-1** Owner decision 2026-11-16 ([#2448](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2448)): original art is given to
  Codex as input, so every generated image, master and baked set is derived from the original: it stays under the ignored
  `local/steamwoven-ui/`, is never committed and never packaged, and follows the
  [HD pack workflow](../workflow/hd-pack.md) for backup. In git: tools, the manifest (identifiers only), the prompt
  library (text) and notes with numbers.
- **SWI-PRV-2** The kit therefore exists only on the owner's devices (and those of a player who builds it from their own
  installation), like the HD pack; it is not shipped with the game. Reopening this needs a new owner decision.
- **SWI-PRV-3** Composites for the owner are posted in the thread and never committed (owner rule 2026-11-11).
- **SWI-PRV-4** Storage of the baked sets (ARC-DEV-2460): `local/steamwoven-ui/baked/<step>/` of the main checkout, written only by `bake.py`; not committed, not packaged and
  not backed up (reproducible from the masters, whose backup convention is in the [toolchain section](../workflow/toolchain.md#steamwoven-kit-bake-arc-dev-2460)). The runtime reads them in place, or from `user://steamwoven-ui/baked` on a player's own device (ARC-UI-2461, [the kit runtime](#the-kit-runtime-arc-ui-2461)).

## Verification

Agent-checkable (acceptance criteria of the slices):

- **SWI-VER-1** Layout model unit tests reproduce the [reference table](#screen-frame-and-scale) exactly (the target model of [#2925](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2925)).
- **SWI-VER-2** The manifest validator passes on the real manifest and fails each synthetic bad manifest.
- **SWI-VER-3** Fit tool tests on synthetic fixtures: exact output size; no halo (in the semi-transparent edge band the
  mean colour difference to the nearest opaque interior is within the bound the tool documents); the colour lock
  leaves the high-frequency energy within 10 % of the input. **As built:** the edge band's mean CIE76 colour difference to the mean of the nearest opaque pixels is at most 3 and its 95th percentile at most 6 (`edge_halo`;
  the gate reads the pipeline's output before the final safety net, and the tests prove it sees the halo of a plain key, over 9, and of a contaminated source when the decontamination is removed; a share of orphan edge pixels above 10 % and an empty master also fail); the high-frequency energy is the standard deviation of L\*, a\* and b\* minus their 7 x 7 box blur over the opaque interior (`hf_energy`), kept within 10 % (`hf_check`, a floor of 0.25 for flat parts);
  `test_fit.py` also holds the exact size for each scale and crop, the colour lock moving the mean of a material to the sheet's within 1 L\*a\*b\* unit, a two-material part locked by region, the refusal of an off-tone generation and of a changed sheet, and the same bytes on a second run.
- **SWI-VER-4** Bake: exact sizes and identical bytes on a second run.
- **SWI-VER-5** Surfaces: seam ratio R ≤ 1.5 at every join inside the composition (a window zone's edge against the strips, the parts of one strip; the measure of
  [ARC-RES-094 U4](../rendering/hd-interface-art.md#9-slice-side-pieces-u4); the designed split of a two-part window is exempt, SWI-BDR-5); there are no sides to match, so the former side-versus-centre statistics are dropped. [#2457](https://github.com/Simarglok/arcanum-steamwoven/issues/2457) may add
  repetition and sharpness bounds for the surfaces it compares ([the study's measures](steamwoven-surface-method-study.md#metrics)); the owner has picked method A (SWI-GEN-5) and added no bounds, so only the seam ratio is a gate, R ≤ 1.5 as above; the owner's 1.8 for the method A strip joins (SWI-GEN-5 condition 4) is a target that the original's own joins (1.82, 1.73) would not meet at 1.5, and the prototype's 0.96 and 1.11 meet both.
- **SWI-VER-6** Smoke captures (local only) at 3840 × 2160, 2560 × 1440, 1920 × 1080, 2560 × 1600, 1920 × 1200,
